## Context

Halcyon Identity operates the customer identity and access management platform that authenticates every end user. It was recorded in the vendor register as an ordinary vendor, and that classification had already survived one review under the closure plan for ML-3 in the 2025 Type I management letter.

## Decision

Halcyon Identity is a subservice organisation, SUB-02, carved out, with three complementary subservice organisation controls. The vendor register is re-reviewed in full against the classification test and the test is written into the vendor onboarding procedure.

## Consequences

Three CSOCs enter the description and a second carve-out has to be explained to readers who expect one. More importantly, this is **ML-3 repeating six weeks after the programme was chartered to fix it**, and the phase records it as a failure caught rather than as diligence performed. A programme that congratulates itself for finding its own known error twice has learned nothing from finding it once.

## Alternatives considered and rejected

Leaving the classification as an ordinary vendor — rejected on the plain application of the test. Using the inclusive method for Halcyon Identity — rejected, because CloudNimbus cannot obtain the evidence an inclusive presentation requires.
