## 1. What this phase is

Phase 04 published the confidentiality and privacy controls, `CNB-C-114` to `CNB-C-133`, and the vendor and
continuity controls, `CNB-C-088` to `CNB-C-093`. Phase 06 reported an availability incident nobody planned.
**Phase 07 reports a retention failure nobody detected**, and it is a different kind of thing: the September
incident lasted seventy-one minutes and was paged in two, while this one lasted **sixty-eight nights** and
was found by an auditor's evidence request.

The phase covers three subjects that a description usually keeps apart and that this business does not.
**Confidentiality** is two criteria and five controls, one of which carries a criterion by itself.
**Privacy** is eighteen criteria and fifteen controls, in a company that collects seven of its twelve
personal-data categories directly from an individual it has no way of contacting. **Third-party assurance**
is eighty-four vendors, eleven sub-processors, two carved-out subservice organisations, and the question
nobody asks in the other direction — what the *other side's* reports require of CloudNimbus.

**The library also grew for the first time.** It has been 148 controls since Phase 04 published it; on
**2026-10-28** it became **149**.

## 2. The vantage — 2026-11-27

**Phase 07 speaks as at 2026-11-27, the last working day before ISO Stage 2 opens on 2026-11-30.** Five
months of the six-month observation window have run, and the period this phase reports is **2026-10-01 to
2026-11-27 — fifty-eight days.** Phases 01 and 02 wrote from 2026-03-31, Phase 03 from 2026-04-30, Phase 04
from 2026-06-30, Phase 05 from 2026-07-31 and Phase 06 from 2026-09-30.

These have happened and are reported in the past tense: the **CAL-08 Q4 vendor and sub-processor review of
2026-10-07**; the **RT-02 retention failure**, discovered 2026-10-23, corrected 2026-10-24, cleared from the
primary stores 2026-10-25 and verified 2026-10-27; the admission of **R-38** to the risk register on
2026-10-23; the admission of **`CNB-C-149`** to the control library on 2026-10-28; the **sub-processor
change effective 2026-11-05**; the **CAL-16 business continuity exercise of 2026-11-12**; and the **annual
notice reconciliation of 2026-11-19**.

**Nothing after 2026-11-27 is stated as an outcome.** **ISO Stage 2 opens in three days and has not
happened** — there is no Stage 2 finding, no nonconformity raised by Northgate Certification Services, and
no certification decision. There is no December, no window close, no examination fieldwork, no sample drawn
by Ashcombe &amp; Doyle, no test exception evaluated, no opinion and no certificate. The **Q4 CAL-06 risk
register review falls on 2026-12-29 and has not been held**, which is why R-38 was admitted **between
quarterly reviews** — permitted expressly by **DEC-306**, and stated here rather than implied.

**Three things happened inside this period and belong to Phase 08**: **ISO Stage 1, 2026-10-12 to 2026-10-14
(MS-14)**; the **second penetration test by Ironwood Security Labs, 2026-10-05 to 2026-10-16, report
received 2026-11-02**; and the **clause 9.2 internal audit of 2026-09-14 to 2026-09-25 and the clause 9.3
management review of 2026-09-30**, already excluded by Phase 06.

**They are named here, in 07.13 §7 and in the evidence index, and — for the penetration test, because
`CNB-C-026`'s annual occurrence is the library's to report — in 07.12 §4.** The claim worth making is not
that they are named once. It is that **nothing is said anywhere in this phase about what any of them
examined or found**: no finding, no observation and no outcome.

## 3. What Phase 07 found, said first

One line each, and the chapter that owns it. Nothing in this section is restated in full here; every figure
below is fixed somewhere else and this is the index to it.

- **A retention rule was not enforced for sixty-eight nights and nothing alerted, because nothing failed.** The migration of 2026-08-17 built a new partitioned relation, copied the rows, truncated the original and swapped the names; the generated RT-02 job resolves its target by an object identifier a rename does not invalidate, so it went on addressing the emptied original and reported a deletion of zero rows on each of the sixty-eight nights to 2026-10-23. **1,847,206 capture points, 58,412 individuals, 34 of the 41 EU-residency customers; the other 7 held none. 34 + 7 = 41.** — **07.03**
- **The examination found it, not the control**, on 2026-10-23 while the Q4 retention evidence sample was being assembled for the service auditor. **A completion record nobody reads is a log, not a control.** — 07.03 §4
- **Forty-one customers were notified on 2026-10-26 where no commitment required it**, over a minuted dissent. — 07.03 §6, GOV-26, ADR-0032
- **The control deviations, and one service commitment failure listed separately from them**, are printed once in full with their populations and corrective actions. SC-09's miss was determined not to be a significant failure for DC4 purposes, and the determination was written down. — **07.12 §5**, ADR-0034, DEC-711
- **`CA-07-01` is a corrective action with no deviation behind it** — reading AWS's own report produced two complementary user entity controls with no owner at CloudNimbus, and no control in the library required anybody to own them. **The absence is the finding.** — `IS-30`, 07.10
- **Two entries in the register changed and no band did.** **R-38** was admitted on 2026-10-23 at 3 × 4 = 12, Moderate; **R-24** was re-rated at GOV-27 on 2026-10-28 from 2 × 4 = 8 to 3 × 4 = 12 on likelihood, because the event it describes occurred. Both are Moderate, so **38 — 8 High · 18 Moderate · 12 Low. 8 + 18 + 12 = 38.** R-06 is held at 4 × 4 = 16 (**DEC-708**). Nothing was closed and nothing was removed. — 07.03 §7
- **The library grew for the first time since Phase 04**, from 148 to **149**, when `CNB-C-149` was admitted on 2026-10-28. **A control admitted in October cannot evidence October the first**: 65 nights in the window, 31 run at this vantage, 0 alerts. — 07.02 §5, ADR-0031

## 7. How to read this phase

Read **07.02** and **07.03** together: the machinery, then what it did. **07.04** is the structural fact
under the whole privacy category — a promise made to a person CloudNimbus cannot reach — and **07.05** is
the coverage gap the programme found by counting its own tenants. **07.10** reads the complementary controls
in the other direction — what the subservice organisations' own reports require of CloudNimbus.
**07.12** is the record and **07.13** says what the phase establishes and what it hands to Phase 08.

## 8. Standing framing

Nothing in this phase is a determination of compliance with any law. **This is the privacy phase and the
temptation is at its worst**, so the position is stated without qualification: no document here says that a
law was engaged or not engaged, that anything was or was not a personal data breach, that a notification was
or was not required by law, or that any processing had or lacked any particular legal basis. What these
documents say is **what the contract says, what the notice says, what management decided and why**. Legal
characterisation belongs to counsel — to Tobias Lund for CloudNimbus and to each customer's own advisers on
their own facts.

The SOC 2 deliverable is a **restricted-use** examination report carrying a CPA firm's **opinion**; there is
no such thing as a SOC 2 certification and nothing here describes CloudNimbus as certified against it. The
ISO deliverable is a **certificate issued by an accredited certification body**, Northgate Certification
Services, Ltd., accredited by ANAB — not by ISO, which certifies nobody. **Points of focus are not
requirements** and **Annex A is not the requirements**; conformity is assessed against clauses 4 to 10. A
SOC 2 finding is a **deviation** or **exception** and an ISO finding is a **nonconformity**; the deviations
this phase records are all also internally identified nonconformities handled under clause 10.2, and **none
has been raised by the certification body, which has not yet audited**. No
fine, penalty or fee amount appears anywhere. All content is fictional and illustrative.
