### The three things it deliberately does not do

It does not draw a **legal conclusion** anywhere — not about the over-retention, not about leave-type
inference, and not about whether any notification was required. It says what the contract says, what the
notice says, what management decided, and why.

It does not report **Stage 1**, the **second penetration test** or September's **internal audit and
management review**, all of which fall inside this period and all of which belong to Phase 08. They are
named once, in 07.00, with nothing attached.

It does not **forecast the opinion** from three deviations here or eight across the window, and it does not
say what Stage 2 will find. Stage 2 opens three days after this vantage.
