## 1. What Phase 07 established

Phase 06 closed on a control that did everything asked of it and measured the wrong thing. **Phase 07 closes
on a control that did everything asked of it and could not see what it was being asked about.** Five
positions, each owned and argued elsewhere and none restated here.

| Position | Where it is owned |
|---|---|
| **RT-02 was not enforced in `eu-central-1` for sixty-eight consecutive nights**, from the partitioning migration of 2026-08-17 to discovery on 2026-10-23, and **1,847,206 geolocation capture points** were retained past their thirteen-month rule. The job ran, deleted nothing and reported success | [07.03](07.03-the-rt-02-retention-failure.md); GOV-26; `D-07-01` |
| **The examination found it, not the control.** The evidence the control produced is the evidence that disproved it, and nobody read it until a service auditor asked for a retention sample | [07.03 §4](07.03-the-rt-02-retention-failure.md) |
| **All 41 EU-residency customers were notified on 2026-10-26 and no commitment required it**, over a minuted dissent | [07.03 §6](07.03-the-rt-02-retention-failure.md); GOV-26; ADR-0032 |
| **The disclosure engages both limbs of DC4** — the first because `CNB-C-127`'s alert condition could not detect the failure mode, the second because **SR-08** was not achieved. Phase 06 engaged the second alone and said the first was not | [07.03 §8](07.03-the-rt-02-retention-failure.md); ADR-0028 |
| **AWS's report states 58 complementary user entity controls and Halcyon Identity's states 19**, 49 and 17 already performed, 7 and 2 not applicable, **two with no owner at all**, and nothing in the library required anybody to read them | [07.10](07.10-reading-the-other-sides-complementary-controls.md); `IS-30`; `CA-07-01` |

**The second DC4 limb is the one worth carrying out of this phase.** "Service commitments **and system
requirements**" is one phrase with two nouns in it, and a description that only ever checks the first will
disclose only the incidents its customers have already complained about.

## 3. The register — 38, and two entries changed

**Entering the period: 37 — 8 High · 17 Moderate · 12 Low. Leaving: 38 — 8 High · 18 Moderate · 12 Low.
8 + 18 + 12 = 38.**

**Two entries changed in the period: R-38 admitted, R-24 re-rated.**

**R-38** was admitted on **2026-10-23** on evidence, between quarterly reviews, which **DEC-306** permits —
owner Devon Ashby, at **3 × 4 = 12, Moderate**. It is not R-06, which describes a job that **fails
silently**; this job did not fail, so **R-06 is held at 4 × 4 = 16** (DEC-708).

**R-24** — geolocation retained or used beyond the purpose the individual was told about, owner **Tobias
Lund** — **moved from 2 × 4 = 8 to 3 × 4 = 12** on likelihood alone: likelihood 2 is "foreseeable but not
expected within the cycle" and the event occurred within the cycle. **DEC-715**;
[GOV-27 §4](governance/GOV-27-q4-privacy-review-and-the-admission-of-cnb-c-149.md) owns both records.

> **A movement that does not change a band is still a movement, and a register that only records band
> changes is a register that has stopped measuring.** R-24's score rose by four and nothing in the summary
> arithmetic moved. Both ratings are Moderate, so the position stays at **38 — 8 High · 18 Moderate · 12
> Low**. The entry, not the total, is where the change is visible.

R-24 was named in Phase 03 as an entry pinned at the eight floor. **It could not move down. Nothing stopped
it moving up, and nobody had asked.** Beyond those two, nothing moved, nothing was closed, nothing removed.

## 4. The library is 149

`CNB-C-149` was admitted on **2026-10-28** at the `CNB-C-133` quarterly privacy review — **DEC-706**,
**GOV-27**, **ADR-0031** — and the library is **149** controls from that date.
[07.02 §5](07.02-the-retention-schedule-and-the-deletion-machinery.md) carries what it is and what it costs:
a window population of **65 nights**, 31 of them run with 0 alerts raised, and an eligible-population test
computed independently of the job that deletes.

**The admission is a relevant change to the system during the period and belongs under DC9**, which Phase 09
carries. It is not DC5's question and it is not DC4.

## 8. What could still go wrong

**The backup residue window has not expired.** The residue from the 2026-10-25 catch-up run **expires
2026-11-29 — two days after this vantage** (DEC-705, ADR-0033), so the RT-02 correction is, at the moment
this phase closes, **not yet finished**.

**`CNB-C-149` will be sampled on 65 nights of a 184-day window**, and a Daily control with a third of the
window behind it is weak evidence about the period however cleanly it runs.

**Two months of the window are reached by no subservice organisation artefact for either party** — one of
them elapsed but for three days, one of them not yet begun. The final bridge letters are requested on
**2026-12-15**, owner Rahul Bhargava, escalating on 2027-01-08, against fieldwork that opens 2027-01-12;
AWS's October letter took thirty-five days to arrive and Halcyon's twenty-eight. **Thirty-five days from
2026-12-15 is 2027-01-19, so on AWS's own precedent the final letter is expected after fieldwork opens** —
which is why the position is stated to Ashcombe &amp; Doyle in advance rather than discovered in fieldwork.

**`CNB-C-008` and `CNB-C-137` are due before 2026-11-30 and had not been performed at this vantage.** Each
has a window population of one, so a slip past the month end is a deviation rate of one hundred per cent.

**And the evidence still has to survive the window.** ML-1 recorded a control whose design was adequate and
whose evidence trail was not. Every claim here resolves later to whether the completion records, the
registers, the vendor readings, the two bridge letters and the notification despatch records are in the
store in the unit 04.12 defined. **Remediating a control is not the same as remediating the evidence trail
it leaves behind** — and the trail here was perfect for sixty-eight nights and said "rows deleted: 0" every
one of them.
