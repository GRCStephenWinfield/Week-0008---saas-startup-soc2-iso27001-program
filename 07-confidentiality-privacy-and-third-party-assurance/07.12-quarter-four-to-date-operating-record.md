## 1. Fifty-eight days, and why that is the number in the heading

06.12 published a quarter. This chapter publishes **fifty-eight days of one** — 2026-10-01 to 2026-11-27 —
because the vantage falls thirty-four days before the quarter ends and the observation window has another
five weeks to run after that.

**That is a weaker artefact than 06.12 and the weakness is stated rather than disguised.** A quarter's
operating record is a closed set: every month in it has ended, every monthly control in it has had its
occurrence, and the arithmetic is final. A record cut on 27 November is a set with one month closed, one
month in progress and one month not begun. Every figure below is exact and none of them is a quarter.

The discipline that follows from that is the same one 06.12 applied at the other end of the window and it
governs the whole chapter: **occurrences to date, and no rate over a population that does not exist yet.**
Where a figure would flatter by being expressed as a proportion of six months, it is not so expressed.

minutes in a 31-day month. `eu-central-1` was 100%.**

**SC-01 was met in both regions in October**, which is worth stating plainly because September's figure was

`us-west-2` carries no production traffic.

**No November figure is given, because November is not a month until it closes.** The temptation to publish
twenty-seven days of it is real — the number would be good — and it is refused on the same reasoning that
refuses a window deviation rate for `CNB-C-098`: a monthly commitment is measured over a month, and a
partial month expressed as a percentage invites a comparison with a figure that means something different.
The November figure will exist on 1 December.

What remains genuinely incomplete is December: the December monthly occurrences, `CNB-C-010` and the other

### 4.1 CAL-07 in the first fortnight

The Q4 access review was completed on **2026-10-09**. Q3's was completed on 2026-07-27, week four of the
quarter, and 06.11 §5 recorded that as an observation rather than a deviation because the control's cadence
is quarterly and the occurrence fell inside the quarter. **The Q4 occurrence is in the first fortnight**,
which is the scheduling discipline `04.11` §3 argues for: a quarterly control scheduled early can slip twice
and still be on time, and one scheduled at the end has no margin at all.

### 4.2 The restore tests, and the number that is still not a rate

`CNB-C-098` was performed on **2026-10-21** and **2026-11-18**. With July's and September's occurrences and
the August one that did not happen, the position is **4 of 5 occurrences to date**.

**That is not a deviation rate and this chapter will not compute one.** The window population is **six**;
December's occurrence has not fallen due. One in five, one in six and twenty per cent are three different
numbers and only one of them will be the honest one, in January, when the population exists. `D-06-01`
stands as Phase 06 recorded it and is not re-opened here; **`IS-19` remains open and irreducible** — the
August occurrence cannot be recovered, and ADR-0026's refusal to re-perform and back-date is the reason the
denominator will always carry it.

### 4.3 `CNB-C-099`, and three resources that were not conforming

The re-scheduled annual re-approval of the shared Terraform module set was performed on **2026-10-20**, and
**three non-conforming resources were rebuilt before sign-off.** The control requires exactly that — the
module set is re-approved annually **with every non-conforming resource rebuilt before sign-off** — so the
three are the control operating rather than a finding against it.

**Zero non-conforming resources would have been the less reassuring result here, and zero divergences is the
reassuring one four chapters earlier — and the difference is not a matter of taste.** A zero is reassuring
when a **preventive** control explains it and unreassuring when nothing does. `CNB-C-125`'s zero divergences
at 07.05 sit downstream of `CNB-C-124`, which fails a pull request introducing a personal-data field absent
from the inventory: the zero is what that gate produces, and a non-zero would mean the gate had been
bypassed. `CNB-C-099`'s re-approval has no such gate in front of it — nothing prevents a resource from
drifting out of conformance between annual re-approvals — so a zero would have meant either that nothing
drifted in a year or that the re-approval was run against the module definitions rather than the estate, and
from outside the two are indistinguishable. It is the same shape as `CNB-C-031`'s four unconfirmed
procedures in September. Three rebuilt resources are evidence that the re-approval reached the estate.

### 4.4 CAL-16, the plan reissue, and A.5.30 — the callback Phase 06 set up

The annual business continuity plan review under **`CNB-C-088`** was performed on **2026-11-05** and the
**CAL-16** business continuity exercise under **`CNB-C-101`** on **2026-11-12**. Both were performed on
time; 06.10 §1 recorded both as not yet due at the September vantage and specifically declined to treat them
as deficiencies, and they are now done.

**Neither of them cites A.5.30.** Both cite **A.5.29**. 06.10 §2 fixed the rule this paragraph is held to —
an Annex A control is answered by the rows that cite it, and a chapter that borrows a nearby row to fill a
limb has written a mapping the library does not contain — and on the library as published only `CNB-C-136`
(planning) and `CNB-C-100` (testing) cite A.5.30. **So the two occurrences a reader would most expect to
close A.5.30's implementation and maintenance limbs do not touch them.** **Two of A.5.30's four limbs —
implementation and maintenance — are still carried by no row that cites it**, which is the position 06.10
§3.2 reached when `IS-24` was widened from one limb to two: the implementation capability is real and
`CNB-C-097` and `CNB-C-099` supply it, but they cite A.8.13 and A.8.14. **`IS-24` stands on both limbs**,
and nothing between here and 2026-12-31 changes it.

### 4.5 The two semi-annual controls Phase 06 handed forward

06.12 §4.3 recorded that `CNB-C-073` and `CNB-C-076` are **the only two semi-annual controls in the entire
Common Criteria set**, that each has a population of exactly **one** in the six-month window, that neither
occurrence fell in Q3, and that both fall here. Both were performed, and both carry a date rather than a
month: **`CNB-C-076`'s tabletop exercise on 2026-10-15** and **`CNB-C-073`'s contact list verification on
2026-11-10**. A row reading "performed in its due month" in a table where every other row carries a date is
the one row a sampler cannot select from, and on the two controls whose window population is one it is the
last place to leave the date out.

**A population of one is the whole of the window's evidence for each of them**, and a single late occurrence
on either would have been a deviation rate of one hundred per cent with nothing in the record to average it
against. That exposure is now resolved in the direction it needed to resolve, and the underlying arithmetic
is unchanged: one occurrence shows that the control operated and cannot show that it operates consistently.

### 4.6 `CNB-C-149`, and a population that starts in October

`CNB-C-149` was admitted to the library on **2026-10-28** and **31 of its 65 nights in the observation window
have run, with 0 alerts raised.**

Two things follow and both belong in an operating record rather than in a summary. **A control admitted in
October cannot evidence October the first** — its window population is 65 nights of 184, and a stated
cadence of Daily will be sampled as what it is. And **zero alerts is an empty population on the alerting
limb**, which is the same weakness this chapter records against `CNB-C-067`'s escalation limb two sections
above: the control has run 31 times and has never yet had to fire.
[07.02 §5](07.02-the-retention-schedule-and-the-deletion-machinery.md) owns what the library's growth to
**149** cost and **ADR-0031** records the decision.

### 4.7 The re-scheduled annual controls of November, and one that reported nothing anywhere

Three of `04.11` §4.2's re-scheduled annual controls fall in November, and none of them was reported
anywhere in this phase until this section.

**`CNB-C-116` — the annual rotation of region-scoped customer-managed keys — was performed on 2026-11-10**,
across all three regions, with the rotation and the re-encryption completion recorded under **EC-04**. Its
window population is **one**. [07.01](07.01-the-confidentiality-criteria-and-the-single-control.md) marks
encryption **Evidenced** and records no deviation against it, and until this occurrence was written down
that status rested on a control the phase had not shown operating. **That is the same failure `06.10`
recorded against A.5.30 and called the clause 7.5 lesson** — a status asserted ahead of the evidence for it
— and naming it here is cheaper than having it named at Stage 2.

**`CNB-C-008` and `CNB-C-137` are due this month and had not been performed at this vantage.** Neither is a
deviation: 2026-11-27 is not 2026-11-30, and a control due in a month that has three days left has not
missed anything. What each has is a **window population of one**, so a slip past 2026-11-30 is a deviation
rate of one hundred per cent with nothing in the record to average it against — the arithmetic `04.11` §3
sets out and the exposure `D-06-05` realised in August on exactly this shape of control.

**Reporting a control that has not yet operated is the point rather than an admission.** The alternative is
the one Phase 06 found with `CNB-C-136`: an annual control re-scheduled into the window, invisible in the
operating record, and discovered to have been missed only when somebody counted the population afterwards.

| `D-07-01` | `CNB-C-126`, `CNB-C-127` | RT-02's generated job matched no rows for 68 consecutive nights after the 2026-08-17 partitioning of the `eu-central-1` table, reporting success each night; 1,847,206 geolocation capture points were retained past their 13-month rule | **68 of 68 nights on 1 of 8 rules in 1 of 3 regions** | **Yes — `CA-07-02`** |
| `D-07-02` | `CNB-C-092` | One Tier 1 vendor held no current assurance artefact at two consecutive quarterly refreshes, 2026-07-30 and 2026-10-07 | **2 of 24** | **Yes — `CA-07-03`** |
| `D-07-03` | `CNB-C-129` | One data subject request assembled in twelve business days against ten | 1 of 31 | **Yes — `CA-07-05`** |

**Three rows, and a fourth that was drafted and does not belong here.** The stale published sub-processor
list was drafted as a deviation against
`CNB-C-120` — a control that operated on its date and **found** the divergence. [07.10
§4](07.10-reading-the-other-sides-complementary-controls.md) says in terms that a finding with no failing
control behind it is a referred issue, and the doctrine was applied to `CA-07-01` and not, at first, to
this. It is **`IS-34`**, referred, with **`CA-07-04`** attached unchanged, and
[07.07 §5](07.07-disclosure-and-the-sub-processor-notice.md) carries it. **The remaining identifiers run
`D-07-01` to `D-07-03` with no hole in them**, the data subject request deviation taking the third place.
Allocating inside a phase's own open series, before the series has been relied on outside the phase, is a
different act from renumbering Phase 06's closed one — which the last paragraph of this section refuses for
the same reason.

**And one service commitment failure, listed separately because it is not a control deviation.**

| ID | Commitment | Failure |
|---|---|---|
| SC-09 | Assistance with a data subject request within 10 business days — O6 | **Not met once in thirty-one.** Received 2026-10-29, due 2026-11-12, delivered 2026-11-16 — twelve business days. Management determined that this was **not a significant failure in the achievement of SC-09 for the purposes of DC4**, and the determination is recorded at **ADR-0034** rather than left implicit |

The separation is the one 06.12 §6 made and it is not presentational. **A control deviation is a control
that did not operate as described; a commitment failure is a promise to a customer that was not kept.** They
are evaluated against different things by different people — the deviations against the applicable trust
services criteria by the service auditor, the commitment against the contract by the customer — and they are
disclosed in different places. `D-07-03` and the SC-09 miss arise from the same request and are two findings
rather than one.

**All three deviations are nonconformities in the ISO sense**, and unlike Phase 06's `D-06-02` none of them
is a design deficiency corrected by amendment alone. The deviation log applies the clause 10.2 test to each
and shows the working.

**Three of the six corrective actions have no deviation above them**, which is a higher proportion than the
phase set out to record and is the consequence of applying 07.10 §4's doctrine consistently. `CA-07-01`
answers the two unowned AWS complementary user entity controls (`IS-30`); `CA-07-04` answers the published
sub-processor list that no control required to be updated at the change (`IS-34`); `CA-07-06` answers the
absence of any comparison between a Tier 1 artefact's period and CloudNimbus's observation window
(`IS-31`, and 07.11 §3 opens it). In each case no control in the library made the promise that was broken.
**Clause 10.2 is engaged by the absence of a requirement as much as by the breach of one**, and filing any
of the three as a deviation to make the paperwork symmetrical would have made the record wrong in order to
make it tidy.

**`D-06-06` is not created.** Phase 06's `D-06-01` to `D-06-05` are closed populations belonging to a closed
period, and anything found now is a Phase 07 deviation with a Phase 07 identifier. Renumbering a previous
phase's series to accommodate a later finding would make both records harder to read and would make the
earlier one look as though it had been revised.

## 6. What this record does not say

**Thirty-four days of the quarter and thirty-four days of the window remain.** Every figure above describes
58 days of a 184-day observation window. `CNB-C-098` has one occurrence left, `CNB-C-149` has 34 nights left
of its 65, `CNB-C-122` has two monthly occurrences left, `CNB-C-008` and `CNB-C-137` are due before
2026-11-30, and the
December-scheduled annual controls have not operated. The service auditor samples the whole six months and
will compute rates over populations that do not exist yet.

**Activities fall inside this period and belong to Phase 08.** They are named in
[07.00](07.00-README.md), in 07.13 §7 and in the evidence index, and `CNB-C-026`'s occurrence appears in
§4's due-and-performed table because the control is the library's — **and nowhere is anything said about
what any of them examined or found.** A claim to name something "once, here" that is untrue four times over
is a claim about tidiness rather than about restraint, and the restraint is real: no finding, no
observation and no outcome from any of them appears in this phase.

**And three deviations here, on top of five in Phase 06, are eight findings and none of them is a conclusion
about the opinion.** Deviations **will be presented for disclosure in Section IV** with the service
auditor's evaluation alongside them — what appears in Section IV is the service auditor's business, not
CloudNimbus's — and the auditor modifies the opinion only where deviations mean a criterion was not met.
That evaluation has not been performed. **Phase 07 does not offer one, does not anticipate one, and would be
wrong to** — ADR-0015's rule applied to an opinion instead of a register, for the third phase running.
