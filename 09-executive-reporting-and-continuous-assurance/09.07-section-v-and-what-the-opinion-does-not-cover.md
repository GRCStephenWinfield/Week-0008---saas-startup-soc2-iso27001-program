## 1. What Section V is, and the sentence on its own face

**Section V is *other information provided by management*. It is not covered by the service auditor's
opinion.** Nothing in it was tested, nothing in it was evaluated, and nothing in it was corrected by
Ashcombe &amp; Doyle LLP — which also means that if anything in it were wrong, no independent party would
have caught it before it reached a reader.

**Management states that on the face of the section itself**, in the section's own opening paragraph. Not in
a footnote, not in the transmittal note, and not by relying on the reader to know which parts of a report an
opinion reaches. **DEC-813** required it in January when the placement was settled, **ADR-0038** carried the
reasoning, and it is repeated here because it is the operative property of everything below.

> A user entity's vendor risk analyst reading five reports in a week does not reliably know that Section V
> sits outside the opinion. **A disclosure whose standing depends on the reader knowing something will be
> misread by exactly the readers who most need it**, and the obligation to say what a section is worth
> belongs to the party writing it.

This chapter is owned by the Chief Financial Officer because the placement decisions were hers — **DEC-813**
on where the collision goes and **DEC-903** on what has to travel with it — and because the section is the
one place in the report where management chose what a reader would see.

## 2. What Section V carries

**Three things, and they are the only three.**

| # | Disclosure | Dates | Why it is here rather than elsewhere |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The **Stage 2 major nonconformity `MAJ-01`**, its correction, its corrective action and its closure | Raised 2026-12-02; closed 2027-01-15 | It is not a component, a commitment, a criterion, a control or an incident of the system, so it is not Section III. Clause 9.2 has no trust services criterion and `CNB-C-146` is ISO-only, so there is no Section IV row for it |
| 2 | The **ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certificate** issued by **Northgate Certification Services, Ltd.**, accredited by **ANAB** | Decision 2027-01-20; issued 2027-01-22; valid to 2030-01-21 | A certification body's decision about a management system with a different boundary. It is not a fact about the described system and no part of the opinion reaches it |
| 3 | The **Statement of Applicability reissue** and the **ISMS scope statement reissue** | 2026-10-30 and 2026-10-28 | Both are ISO artefacts, both fall inside the observation window, and **neither is a change to the system described in a SOC 2 report** — which is why `09.03` §5 keeps them out of DC9 |

**The major nonconformity is stated in Northgate's terms rather than CloudNimbus's.** It was raised on
2026-12-02, the third day of a Stage 2 audit that ran 2026-11-30 to 2026-12-04 — **inside the observation
window**, twenty-nine days before it closed. Clause 9.2.1 requires internal audits at planned intervals to
provide information on whether the management system conforms to the organisation's own requirements **and**
to the requirements of the standard; clause 9.2.2 a) requires the criteria and scope to be defined for each
audit. The internal audit of 2026-09-14 to 09-25 covered the 91 Annex A controls the Statement of
Applicability determines necessary and **did not cover clauses 4 to 10, and did not cover `eu-central-1`**.
The same failure engages **A.5.22**, because the internal audit is a supplier service whose scope CloudNimbus
defined and never reviewed. **One nonconformity, not two: outsourcing the work did not outsource the
accountability.**

**And the disclosure carries what happened next**, because a disclosure that stops at the finding is worse
than none. Accepted without contest at the closing meeting of 2026-12-04. Correction and corrective action
plan submitted **2026-12-19**, inside the twenty-day limit **Northgate set** — a limit ISO/IEC 17021-1
requires a certification body to define rather than one the standard fixes. A full clause 4 to 10 and
`eu-central-1` audit conducted **2027-01-05 to 01-09** under a scope CloudNimbus rewrote. Supplementary
audit **2027-01-13**; closure verified **2027-01-15**; certification decision **2027-01-20**; certificate
issued **2027-01-22**. **`CNB-C-150` is named there as an ISO corrective action with no population inside
the observation window** — the point of naming it being that it cannot have improved anything the report
says.

**The two reissues are stated with what each was and what it was not.** The Statement of Applicability
records which Annex A controls are necessary under clause 6.1.3 d) — **91 of 93 determined necessary, with
A.7.11 and A.7.12 the two determined not necessary and excluded with justification** — and was reissued on
2026-10-30. The ISMS scope statement, the clause 4.3 boundary, was reissued on 2026-10-28 under **DEC-804**
and Northgate was notified inside the thirty days **O10** requires. The Statement of Applicability reissue
was assessed against O10 and determined **not** to be a change to the ISMS, its scope or the organisation.

## 3. Both, or neither

**Section V could have carried the certificate alone.** Nothing in the attestation standards obliges an
entity to put anything in Section V; it is the section whose whole premise is that management chose to put
information there.

> **It carries the certificate and the major nonconformity together, because including one without the
> other is selective disclosure** — and a section where management chooses the contents is a section where
> **selectivity is the only thing a reader can measure.** A Section V holding a certificate dated 2027-01-22
> and nothing else would be a true document that had answered a question nobody asked while leaving
> unanswered the one a reader who noticed the dates would ask: what happened between a Stage 2 audit inside
> the observation window and a certificate seven weeks later.
>
> The converse holds and was tested rather than assumed. **A Section V carrying the major nonconformity and
> not the certificate would be equally selective**, and would leave a reader with an unresolved finding
> against an entity that had in fact closed it in forty-four days. **The test applied was whether the
> placement produces the same answer when the fact is flattering and when it is not**, and it does: both are
> information a user of the report should have, neither is part of the described system, and neither is
> covered by the opinion.
>
> **DEC-903**, taken by Marisol Vega on 2027-02-09. **ADR-0042** records the decision, the alternatives and
> the rule it establishes: **the two travel together or neither travels.**

**The rule is written to bind the next report as well as this one**, which is the only form in which it is
worth anything. In 2028, **if the certificate has been maintained through surveillance**, it will be a fact
of a different kind and the nonconformity will be two years old, and the temptation to carry the first
without the second will be larger rather than smaller. The surveillance audit is scheduled for 2027-11 and
this record says nothing about what it will find.

## 4. The restraint that goes with it

**Section V is not a marketing surface.** It exists so a user entity has information the opinion does not
cover, and everything in it is stated with the same flatness.

**The certificate gets no adjective.** It is described by issuer, accreditation, standard, scope, date and
validity, and by nothing else. It is not *achieved*, not *awarded*, not *hard-won* and not held up against
the fourteen months behind it. **ISO does not certify anyone**; Northgate Certification Services, Ltd. took
the decision, under ANAB accreditation, against ISO/IEC 17021-1 and ISO/IEC 27006-1:2024.

**The nonconformity gets no mitigation.** It is stated as Northgate stated it, from the finding statement
retained at **EV-811** rather than from a CloudNimbus summary of it, and the fact that four of the five
Stage 2 findings were already in CloudNimbus's own records is **not** offered in the section. That fact is
true, it is in `08.12`, and it does not belong in a disclosure whose purpose is to tell a reader what an
independent body found.

**And the section says what was not closed.** The four minor nonconformities' corrective action plans were
accepted by Northgate on 2026-12-19 and **their implementation is verified at the first surveillance audit,
scheduled for 2027-11** — not before, not by this report, and not by anything CloudNimbus can do in the
interval. A Section V that listed the major as closed and left the reader to assume the same of the minors
would have used a closure as a claim.

**Two things were considered for Section V and refused.** The **penetration test results** — sixteen
findings in May and nine in October, all remediated — were refused because a report of testing performed by
a party CloudNimbus engaged, disclosed in a section nobody examines, reads as evidence and is not evidence;
a reader cannot tell a comprehensive test from a narrow one. And a **summary of the 2027 position** was
refused because the 2027 window was six weeks old when the description was finalised and had produced no
population, no rate and no outcome. **Neither refusal is about confidentiality. Both are about what an
unexamined section can honestly carry.**

## 5. What Section V does not do

**It does not extend the opinion.** Nothing in it is tested and nothing in it is opined on. A reader who
takes the certificate's presence in the report as an auditor's view of the certificate has read a section
that says on its face that it is not.

**It does not convert one framework into the other.** The certificate is a conformity assessment of a
management system against clauses 4 to 10, by audit, at the opening of a three-year cycle. The report is a
practitioner's opinion on the suitability of design and operating effectiveness of specified controls
throughout a stated period. A section that carries both facts in one document has still not produced a
combined deliverable — **`09.04` §3 owns that argument** — and `ADR-0001` refused the blur in January 2026.
This section is where the refusal would have been easiest to abandon.

**It does not make `MAJ-01` a test exception.** **"Nonconformity" is ISO and "exception" or "deviation" is
SOC 2.** The same fact can be both, either or neither; this one is a nonconformity and not an exception,
because clause 9.2 has no criterion and `CNB-C-146` appears in no criterion's control set. **Calling it an
exception is a single word that would produce a document saying something untrue about which independent
party concluded what.**

**And it does not reach any legal conclusion.** Neither the report nor the certificate is a determination of
compliance with any law — not the GDPR, not the CCPA or CPRA, not any state privacy statute — and Section V
says so in the same terms every phase of this programme has.
