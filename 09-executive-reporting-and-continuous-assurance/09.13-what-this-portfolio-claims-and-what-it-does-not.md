## 1. What the programme produced

Five things, stated flatly.

**An unmodified opinion** from Ashcombe &amp; Doyle LLP on an examination across all five trust services
categories for the period 2026-07-01 to 2026-12-31, with **nine test exceptions disclosed in Section IV**
under nine of the sixty-one applicable criteria.

**An ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certificate**, issued 2027-01-22 by Northgate Certification Services, Ltd., an
ANAB-accredited certification body, covering the whole organisation and valid to 2030-01-21.

**One major nonconformity raised, accepted at the closing meeting without contest, and closed in forty-four
days** — 2026-12-02 to 2027-01-15, with the reasoning for not arguing it down recorded rather than the
outcome alone.

**A risk register that grew twice on evidence, closed nothing, removed nothing and ended with no High
entries** — 38 entries, 0 High · 17 Moderate · 21 Low.

**Seven of eight objectives met**, and the eighth, OBJ-03, missed at 16.9% against a 70% target.

That is the whole of it. Everything below is what those five things do not mean.

## 2. What this portfolio does not claim

**Not that CloudNimbus is secure**, which neither instrument measures — **09.04 §3** owns that argument and
says what security is a property of and how far either document sits from it.

**Not that the controls will operate in 2027.** A report is a statement about a period that has ended. The
2027 observation window is ten weeks old on this vantage, the surveillance audit is eight months away, and
the strongest thing anyone can say about the next twelve months is that the calendar exists and the controls
have owners.

**Not that a clean report predicts anything.** The evidence is inside the report itself: the RT-02 deletion
job addressed an emptied relation for sixty-eight consecutive nights, reporting success and deleting
nothing, **eleven weeks before the period ended** and in the period an unmodified opinion was expressed on.
Nothing in the control library detected it; it was found because somebody was assembling a retention
evidence sample for the examination. **A report that contains that failure is not a report that would have
predicted it**, and any reading of an unmodified opinion as forward-looking has to explain away its own
contents.

**Not that "clean opinion" means zero exceptions.** It does not, the standards do not use the phrase, and
this portfolio has said so since 01.02. Nine exceptions are disclosed; the service auditor considered
whether one required a modification and concluded it did not, and 09.05 reproduces the reasoning rather than
the conclusion — a portfolio that showed the outcome and not the reasoning would teach a reader that
unmodified opinions happen.

**Not that the certificate and the report say similar things.** One is a conformity assessment of a
management system against clauses 4 to 10, by audit at the **opening** of a three-year cycle, with Annex A
consulted as a reference set under clause 6.1.3 c) and dispositioned in a Statement of Applicability that
determined 91 of 93 controls necessary. The other is a practitioner's opinion on the suitability of design
and the operating effectiveness of specified controls **throughout a stated period**, with the tests and the
results printed. **They answer different questions and neither substitutes for the other**, and **09.04 §3**
says why there is no third thing the two combine into.

**Not that the programme found everything.** Northgate raised five findings and CloudNimbus's own records
already contained four of them — `MIN-01` was Camberwell's `NC-INT-01`, `MIN-02` was Phase 06's `IS-24`,
`MIN-03` was Camberwell's `NC-INT-02` partially closed, and `MIN-04` was Phase 07's `D-07-02`. **The fifth,
the major, was not in the records as a finding. It was in them as a decision** — read, agreed, minuted at
the management review and deferred twice. An entity that failed to notice a gap has a detection problem; an
entity that noticed it, took it to its highest governance body and decided to leave it has a different one,
and the second is not the kind that monitoring evidence answers.

**And not that any of this is a determination of compliance with any law.** Not the GDPR, not the CCPA or
CPRA, not any state privacy statute. That sentence has appeared in every phase since the first and is not a
disclaimer bolted to the end: it is the inference a reader is most likely to draw unprompted.

## 3. The four failures, and what each taught

**RT-02.** A control whose operating condition is that a record was written, rather than what the record
says. The deletion job ran every night, completed successfully and wrote a completion record every time —
against a relation the August partitioning migration had emptied. Success and non-existence produced the
same observable state, which is precisely what `02.12` §4 said must not happen. `IS-33` still owes a number:
**the library has not been enumerated for controls with that shape**, and nobody knows how many there are.

**`CNB-C-096`.** A control that says how often and never says what. It fixed cadence, regions and the paging
condition and was silent on what the synthetic probe must exercise. The probe performed a read, so through
seventy-one minutes in which reads succeeded and writes failed, every word of the control was satisfied and
no burn registered against the error budget. **Detection worked; measurement did not.** `CNB-C-068` beside
it was explicit about the transactions it exercised, which makes the silence a design deficiency rather than
an inevitability: the same library contained the pattern that would have prevented it.

**The missed restore test.** A correction that fixes the instances is not a corrective action, and clause
10.2 says so in words that distinguish the two. The August occurrence did not happen; it was not
re-performed and back-dated; the deviation stands at 1 of 6 and `IS-19` records that the occurrence is
unrecoverable. The temptation to repair a denominator arrived three times in three phases and was refused
three times, under ADR-0026, ADR-0040 and the reasoning `CNB-C-136`'s December performance carries. **An
occurrence is an event on a date.**

**The major.** The control library got ahead of the practice it described, and **reading the library told
nobody anything was wrong.** The audit programme document was written in January 2026; `CNB-C-146` was
written in June and describes a programme nobody brought the document up to. Both were correct on their own
face and nothing compared them. `IS-35` records the class rather than the instance: a control statement and
the procedure that carries it drifting apart silently, with no signal from the library side, can exist
anywhere in 150 rows and has not been enumerated.

## 4. And the one about the portfolio itself

Every phase of this programme was read adversarially before it was issued, and **every one found real
defects: 19, 29, 26, 33, 34, 74, 77 and 46** across the eight phases that had been issued before this one.
That is **338 findings**. This phase's count is not in the list, for the obvious reason: it is being read
now, and a phase that published its own defect count before the reading would be publishing an estimate.

The defects were not cosmetic and are worth characterising rather than totalling. **Three phases had to be
corrected for stating one argument four and five times**, which is why this phase carries a
one-argument-one-owner table at the front and why no chapter restates what another owns. **Two described a
mechanism that was technically impossible. One quoted a trust services criterion in wording superseded in
2017.** Others were arithmetic that did not close, a denominator quoted without its population, and a
control cited against a criterion the library did not map it to.

**A programme that finds its own defects is not a programme without defects, and the count is the evidence
for the method rather than against it.** The alternative — nine phases written and nothing found — would say
only that nobody looked. That is the same argument the zero on the security incident row rests on: a zero
produced by a detective control with a five-figure population is a different kind of zero from one produced
by silence, and a defect count of zero would be the second kind.

**The uncomfortable version of that argument is worth ending on.** The largest count belongs to Phase 07, at
77, and Phase 07 describes the failure the service auditor nearly modified the opinion over. One reading is
that the hardest material attracts the most correction, which is flattering; the other is that the hardest
material was written least well the first time, which is not. **Both are consistent with the record, this
document does not choose between them, and the count is published either way.**

## 5. What is left

The programme is over and the work is not. Sixteen corrective actions are open, and beside them the
correction audit's **clause 7.4 minor nonconformity**, open with its clause 10.2 disposition and no closure
— 09.12 §2.1. **Seventeen issues are open** — `IS-19`, `IS-20`, `IS-22`, `IS-23`, `IS-25`, `IS-26`, `IS-27`,
`IS-29`, `IS-30`, `IS-32` to `IS-39` — and two of them owe a number nobody has produced. The register holds
thirty-eight entries never reduced by closure. **The 2027 window has 295 days left in it**, 2027-03-11 to
2027-12-31. The first surveillance audit is a date in a calendar.

**None of that is a shortfall against the plan.** It is what the end of a first cycle looks like when it is
described accurately, and the only claim this portfolio makes for itself is that it described it accurately.
01.07 §7 set that as the fifth success criterion, said it could not be measured, and stated it anyway. It
still cannot be measured. **The four beside it that can be are scored at 09.11 §6, where the third is reported
not met** — and that section was written because a reader asked why the charter's own test had gone unmarked
while eight objectives were being reported. What can be checked is whether the one objective that was missed
is reported as missed, whether the exceptions are printed with their populations, whether the forecast is
marked against the actual in both directions, and whether anything closed in the last fortnight that should
not have. **A reader who checks those four things has the whole of the argument for this programme, and does
not have to take a word of it on trust.**
