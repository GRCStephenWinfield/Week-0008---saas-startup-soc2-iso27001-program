## 1. The report as issued

**Ashcombe &amp; Doyle LLP issued the report on 2027-02-26.** It is **MS-18**, the last milestone in the
programme's delivery plan, and it arrived on the date `01.12` set for it in January 2026. Engagement partner
**Meredith Vance, CPA**; senior manager **Dev Ramanathan**; fieldwork 2027-01-12 to 2027-02-05.

It is an **examination report** under the AICPA attestation standards — **AT-C section 105**, the concepts
common to all attestation engagements, and **AT-C section 205**, *Assertion-Based Examination Engagements*.
It is not a certificate, there is no certification behind it, and no document in this programme has ever
described CloudNimbus as certified against it.

**It has five sections and three authors.**

| Section | What it carries | Whose it is |
|---|---|---|
| **I** | The service auditor's report, including the opinion, the basis for it, the respective responsibilities, the inherent limitations and the restriction on use | **Ashcombe &amp; Doyle LLP** |
| **II** | Management's written assertion | **CloudNimbus management** |
| **III** | The description of the system | **CloudNimbus management** |
| **IV** | The applicable trust services criteria, the related controls, the tests performed and the results | **Ashcombe &amp; Doyle LLP** |
| **V** | Other information provided by management, **not covered by the opinion** | **CloudNimbus management** |

**The opinion in Section I reaches Sections II, III and IV and stops.** Section V sits outside it, and
management says so on the face of Section V rather than leaving a reader to infer it from the structure.
`09.07` carries that section and `ADR-0042` the reasoning.

## 2. What the opinion says, in three limbs

**Stated once, here, and not restated anywhere in this phase.**

> In the service auditor's opinion, **in all material respects**, for the period **2026-07-01 to
> 2026-12-31**:
>
> **(a)** the description presents the CloudNimbus Workforce Platform that was designed and implemented
> throughout the period in accordance with the description criteria;
>
> **(b)** the controls stated in the description were **suitably designed** throughout the period to provide
> reasonable assurance that the applicable trust services criteria would be achieved **if the controls
> operated as described** and if the complementary user entity controls and the complementary subservice
> organisation controls assumed in the design operated effectively; and
>
> **(c)** the controls stated in the description **operated effectively** throughout the period to provide
> reasonable assurance that the applicable trust services criteria were achieved.
>
> **The opinion is unmodified.**

Three limbs, and each is capable of failing on its own. Limb (a) is about a document; **a description can be
truthful and still fail it, by omitting something DC section 200 requires.** Limb (b) is a design question
answered on a counterfactual — *if* the controls operated as described — and it is the limb the two
carve-outs and the eleven complementary user entity controls hang off. Limb (c) is the one a Type II exists
for and the one a Type I cannot reach: **a Type I opines on suitability of design as of a date, performs no
tests of operating effectiveness, and therefore cannot produce a test exception at all.** The 2025 Pinehurst
report was a Type I on Security alone, and its clean result is the reason `01.04` calls it the spine of this
programme rather than a precedent for it.

**Four forms of opinion were available and each would have meant something specific here.**

| Form | What it would have meant in this engagement |
|---|---|
| **Unmodified** | The deviations disclosed in Section IV do not mean the applicable criteria were not achieved for the period taken as a whole. **This is the form issued** |
| **Qualified** | *Except for* a stated matter, the criteria were achieved. **This was the live alternative**: a qualification in respect of **P4.2** on the sixty-eight nights of exception 5. `09.05` sets out the reasoning both ways |
| **Adverse** | The description is not fairly presented, or the controls did not operate effectively, in a way that is both material and pervasive. Nothing in this engagement approached it |
| **Disclaimer** | The practitioner could not obtain sufficient appropriate evidence to form an opinion. The nearest candidate was the two months of the window reached by no artefact from either subservice organisation — volunteered on the second day of fieldwork, resolved before testing closed, and never a scope limitation |

**Exceptions do not modify an opinion. Evaluations do.** Nine test exceptions are disclosed in Section IV,
and the practitioner modifies only where the deviations mean the applicable trust services criteria were
**not** achieved. A report carrying exceptions and an unmodified opinion is an ordinary combination; so is a
report carrying exceptions and a qualified one. **The count is not the test and was never going to be.**

## 3. What an unmodified opinion is not

**This section is longer than the one above it, deliberately.** The three limbs take a paragraph to state
and a career to misread, and every misreading this programme has met in fourteen months of customer
conversations is a misreading of what the opinion does **not** say.

**It is not a statement that nothing went wrong.** **Nine test exceptions are disclosed in Section IV of
this report, under nine of the sixty-one applicable criteria**, and every one of them is a fact about a
population or a sample the practitioner tested. One monthly restore test was not performed. Two emergency
changes were retrospectively approved late. A retention rule addressed an emptied relation for sixty-eight
consecutive nights, reporting success and deleting nothing, in the region holding the most sensitive
personal information the platform carries. **All of that is inside a report whose opinion is unmodified**,
because the opinion is a conclusion about whether criteria were achieved over a period and not a summary of
whether the period was uneventful.

**It is not a statement about any period other than the six months named.** The opinion attaches to
2026-07-01 to 2026-12-31. It says nothing about January 2027, nothing about the state of the system on the
day a reader opens the report, and nothing about the 2027 observation window, which was ten weeks old at
this vantage and had produced no rate, no deviation and no position. **A report is a statement about a
period that has ended.** The instrument for the interval after it is the bridge letter at `09.09`, which is
management-issued, unaudited, and carries no practitioner opinion of any kind.

**It is not a statement about CloudNimbus's security.** Security is not a thing an examination measures. An
examination measures whether specified controls, described by management, were suitably designed and
operated effectively throughout a stated period to provide reasonable assurance that specified criteria were
achieved; a certification audit assesses whether a management system conforms to clauses 4 to 10.
**Security is a property of an organisation on a day, against an adversary nobody has met**, and the
distance between it and either document is not a matter of degree. **A vendor risk analyst who reads an
unmodified opinion as a verdict on whether the platform can be broken into has read a document that does not
contain one**, and the evidence for that is inside the report: the Critical tenant isolation finding of May
2026 was found by a penetration test, remediated and retested clean **before the window opened**, and appears
in the report nowhere at all.

**It is not transferable.** The opinion attaches to **these** controls, in **this** description, over
**this** period, on the criteria management selected. It does not travel to a different service, a different
period, a different scope, or a sibling entity. It does not travel to the corporate back-office systems that
`02.02` §5 excluded, or to the professional services offering, or to the non-production environments whose
exclusion rests on **SR-12**.

**And it is not the certificate.** The ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certificate issued on 2027-01-22 by Northgate
Certification Services, Ltd. is a certification body's decision about a management system assessed against
clauses 4 to 10, at the opening of a three-year cycle. The report is a practitioner's opinion on the
suitability of design and operating effectiveness of specified controls throughout a stated period, with the
tests and the results printed. **They answer different questions, neither substitutes for the other, and
there is no combined deliverable.** `09.13` says what neither of them claims.

> **"Clean opinion" is not a term the standards use.** There is no such form. The forms are unmodified,
> qualified, adverse and disclaimer, and none of them carries an adjective about tidiness. The phrase is a
> sales-conversation compression of *unmodified*, and it has picked up a second meaning on the way — **zero
> exceptions** — which the standards do not support and which this report contradicts on its own face.
>
> A reader who has learned the phrase from a procurement call will open this report expecting Section IV to
> be empty, find nine entries, and conclude that something has gone wrong with either the report or the
> vendor. **That is why the report's own first page is Section I and not a summary**, why the covering note
> accompanying all 155 deliveries names the number nine rather than waiting to be asked, and why this
> programme has never once written the phrase except to refuse it.

## 4. The report date is also the date through which subsequent events were considered

**2027-02-26 does two jobs and the second is easy to miss.** It is the date the report was issued, and it is
the date through which the practitioner considered events occurring after the period end that might require
adjustment to, or disclosure in, the description.

That is why a report about a period ending 2026-12-31 contains facts dated January 2027. **`CNB-C-150` was
admitted to the control library on 2027-01-08** and **the certificate was issued on 2027-01-22**; both were
represented to the practitioner in the representation letter as subsequent events, and both are visible in
the report — `CNB-C-150` and the certificate in Section V, neither in the description's DC9 disclosure.

**Neither is in the tested population and neither could be.** `CNB-C-150` was admitted eight days after the
window closed, has no occurrence inside it, and cannot have improved anything the report says about
2026-07-01 to 2026-12-31. `ADR-0040` recorded that on the day the control was admitted rather than being
asked about it in fieldwork. **A control that did not exist in a period is not evidence about that period**,
and a report that let a reader infer otherwise would be using a disclosure as a claim.

## 5. What this chapter does not do

**It does not reproduce the report.** The report is **restricted-use**, provided under non-disclosure on
request, and this chapter is a programme record about it rather than a copy of it. `09.08` sets out who the
intended users are.

**It does not explain the exceptions.** Section IV carries the criterion, the control as stated in the
description, the test performed, the population, the result and the service auditor's evaluation, and
`09.06` republishes all six columns' worth. Nothing about them belongs here.

**It does not argue the near-modification.** The engagement team raised on 2027-01-28 that it was
considering whether exception 5 required a modification in respect of P4.2, and the reasoning that resolved
it is `09.05`'s and is set out there in full, including the parts that point the other way.

**And it does not treat an unmodified opinion as an achievement.** The form of the opinion is another
party's conclusion, formed on that party's procedures and judgement. `01.07` §3.1 said in January 2026 that
**OBJ-01 should never have been written as a target for that reason**, and `09.11` scores it accordingly:
met, on an objective the charter itself disowned.
