### The five things this phase deliberately does not do

It does not **congratulate**. The report is issued and the certificate is on the wall, and the close-out
says what each is worth and what neither covers.

It does not **close a corrective action to make the close-out tidy** — sixteen are open and sixteen are
reported open, including three with an argument for closure that was refused. **ADR-0045.**

It does not **restate OBJ-03's measure and then declare the objective met**. The miss is reported first, in
its own words, and the better measure comes after — and the phase records that the better measure was found
by an adversarial reader and not by the people who wrote the objective.

It does not **claim the outcome was earned rather than reasoned**. The distance between an unmodified
opinion and a qualified one, in this engagement, was one judgement about one denominator, and 09.05 says so.

And it does not **claim the programme found everything**. Northgate found four things CloudNimbus had
already found and one it had decided about.
