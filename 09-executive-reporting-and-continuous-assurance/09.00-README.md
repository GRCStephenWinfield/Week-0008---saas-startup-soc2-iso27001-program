## 1. What this phase is

**Both deliverables exist.** The report was issued on 2027-02-26 with an unmodified opinion and nine test
exceptions disclosed in Section IV. The certificate was issued on 2027-01-22 and hangs on a wall in Denver.
**This is the phase in which a portfolio congratulates itself, and this one does not.**

What the phase does instead is say what each document is worth, who wrote which part of it, who may read it,
and what it does not cover — then mark the programme against what it said about itself fourteen months ago.
**Seven of eight objectives were met and one was missed badly, and the missed one is the objective the whole
programme was designed around.**

Two arguments carry the phase. The first is **structural**: a SOC 2 report has five sections and three
authors, and almost every misreading of one is a misreading of which. The second is **temporal**: a change
before the period, during it and after it produces three different disclosures. **Both are rules about where
a fact belongs.**

## 2. The vantage — 2027-03-11

**Phase 09 speaks as at 2027-03-11, the day of the board and Audit &amp; Risk Committee report.**

Reportable in full and in the past tense: management's **written assertion** and the **representation
letter**, both 2027-02-26; the **description of the system** including **DC9**; the **report issued
2027-02-26** (MS-18) and **the opinion**; **Section IV**'s nine exceptions as published with the service
auditor's evaluation of each; **Section V**; the **distribution under O2**, completed 2027-03-06; **nine
bridge letters issued under O3**; the **March CAL-06 register review** of 2027-03-09; and the **board and
Audit &amp; Risk Committee report** of 2027-03-11.

**Nothing after 2027-03-11 is an outcome.** The **first surveillance audit is scheduled for 2027-11**,
within twelve months of the certification decision of 2027-01-20 as **O11** requires, and it has not
happened. The **2027 observation window runs 2027-01-01 to 2027-12-31 and is ten weeks old**; it has
produced no population, no rate and no position, and no document here states one. Recertification falls in
year three, against a certificate valid to 2030-01-21.

## 3. What Phase 09 reports, said first

Five things, named here and argued elsewhere. **Nothing in this section is restated in full anywhere in this
README.**

- **The opinion is unmodified, and nine test exceptions are disclosed in Section IV.** The two facts are not
  in tension: an exception is a fact about a tested population, and the practitioner modifies only where the
  deviations mean the applicable criteria were not achieved. **"Clean opinion" is not a term the standards
  use.** — **09.04**, **09.06**
- **The service auditor considered modifying the opinion over exception 5 and did not, and it was close.**
  The reasoning is published against the outcome, including the parts that point the other way, and a
  different engagement team on the same facts could have reached the other answer. — **09.05**, ADR-0041
- **Section V carries the certificate and the Stage 2 major nonconformity together.** Including one without
  the other is selective disclosure, and Section V is the one section where selectivity is the only thing a
  reader can measure. **It is not covered by the opinion, and management states that on its face.** —
  **09.07**, ADR-0042, DEC-903
- **The Phase 03 forecast was right at the band level and wrong five times underneath it.** 0 · 16 · 22
  against 0 · 17 · 21, with eleven of thirty-eight ratings diverging. — **09.10**
- **OBJ-03 was missed at 16.9% against a 70% target, and sixteen corrective actions are open and reported
  open.** Nothing was closed to make the close-out tidy. — **09.11**, **09.12**, ADR-0043, ADR-0045

## 8. How to read this phase

Read **09.01** to **09.07** in order: they follow the report's own structure, from the assertion management
signs, through the description it is made about, to the sections the opinion reaches and the one it does
not. **09.05** is the centrepiece and needs `09.04` §2 in front of it. **09.08** and **09.09** are about who
may read the document and what covers the gap after it; **09.10** to **09.12** mark the programme against
what it forecast, what it targeted and what it leaves running; **09.13** says what none of it claims.

## 9. Standing framing

**A SOC 2 examination produces an opinion and never a certificate.** No certificate exists against it and
nobody holds one, and the deliverable is a **restricted-use** examination report intended for management,
for user entities during the period, and for other specified parties with sufficient knowledge and
understanding. **It is provided under non-disclosure on request and is not a general-use document.**

**ISO does not certify anyone.** The certificate of 2027-01-22 was issued by **Northgate Certification
Services, Ltd.**, accredited by **ANAB** under **ISO/IEC 17021-1** and **ISO/IEC 27006-1:2024**.

**Points of focus are not requirements. Annex A is not the requirements**; conformity is assessed against
clauses 4 to 10, with Annex A reached through clause 6.1.3 c) as a comparison verifying that no necessary
control has been omitted. **"Nonconformity" is ISO and "exception" or "deviation" is SOC 2**, and this phase
publishes both in one report without swapping them. **A bridge letter is management-issued and unaudited.**

**Neither deliverable is a determination of compliance with any law**, and no document here reaches a legal
conclusion. No fine or penalty amount appears anywhere; programme cost and professional fees are published
and are not penalties. All content is fictional and illustrative.
