## 5. Why nothing moved

Three reasons, each about a population rather than about a preference.

**The entries whose events occurred in the fourth quarter have at most three months behind them.** R-09's
two provisioning deviations were found on 2026-12-18 and R-10's three late revocations on 2026-12-29.
`CA-08-01` and `CA-08-02` address them, and neither has produced an occurrence under the corrected
arrangement that this review could look at. Moving an entry on ten weeks of a corrected path is the error
DEC-810 refused in December on six.

**Ten entries are at their floors**, which is what §3's Basis column says on ten rows and what this section
has to agree with. **R-01, R-02, R-03, R-07, R-19, R-20 and R-37** sit at 2 × 5 = 10 with nowhere to go: the
next likelihood step is 1, reserved for the not-reasonably-foreseeable, and no consequence has changed.
**R-05, R-11 and R-12** sit at 2 × 4 = 8, and eight is a floor downward. Those ten had nowhere to go before
the meeting opened, and R-24 is the standing reminder that a floor stops an entry going down and stops
nothing going up.

**And the 2027 observation window is ten weeks old.** Sixty-eight days of operating record, against controls
whose cadences are monthly, quarterly and annual. **The first review of a new cycle has nothing to move on**,
and saying so is the same discipline that made the December movement defensible: every review from June to
September stated on the record that it was waiting for a population, and the review that moved nineteen
entries is only honest if the review after it is willing to move none.

## 6. The two proposals, and why both were declined

**A review at which nobody proposes anything is not a review**, and two reductions were put.

**R-18 — an availability incident breaches the 99.9% monthly commitment — proposed for reduction from
3 × 3 = 9 to 2 × 3 = 6**, on the ground that no Severity-1 or Severity-2 incident has been declared since
2026-09-08 and that January and February 2027 were both inside SC-01 in both regions. **Declined.** Its
described event occurred inside the period the register is reporting, six months of clean months are what
the register expects rather than evidence against the entry, and two months of a new window is not a period.
The proposal was Wes Delacroix's and the decline was his as well, on the chair's question about what
population two months represents.

**R-09 — access is provisioned before the manager approval that authorises it is recorded — proposed for
reduction from 4 × 3 = 12 to 3 × 3 = 9**, on the ground that `CA-08-01`'s gate has been deployed.
**Declined, and the reason is the register's oldest rule.** A deployed gate is a treatment item, not a
population. GOV-32's Basis column exists precisely so that a movement supported by *TP-14 complete* is sent
back and a movement supported by *312 changes, 40 tested, 2 deviations* is taken. **R-09's event occurred
twice in a population tested in full, eleven weeks ago, and nothing has provisioned access through the new
gate in a volume this review could look at.**

**Neither decline is a criticism of the proposal.** Both were made by risk owners looking at genuine
evidence, and a register whose owners stop proposing reductions has stopped being maintained in a different
and worse way than one whose reviews decline them.

## 7. What this review did not do

**It admitted no new risk.** Nothing in the ten weeks disproved an assumption the register does not already
carry, and ADR-0029's doctrine holds: a register that grows every time something happens is a log, not a
register. **`IS-37` is a programme issue about the register's own maintenance and is not an entry in it.**

**It closed nothing and removed nothing.** Thirty-eight entries entered and thirty-eight left. **ADR-0045**
records the standing position for the whole close-out.

**It performed no comparison against the Phase 03 close forecast.** 0 · 16 · 22 against 0 · 17 · 21 is
**09.10**'s, entry by entry and in both directions, and a review that both produced a position and graded it
against a forecast would be doing two jobs in one minute — which is the reason GOV-32 refused the same thing
in December.

**And it made no statement about the examination or the certificate.** A risk register is management's
instrument, no rating in it appears in Section IV, and nothing decided at this meeting bears on either
deliverable.
