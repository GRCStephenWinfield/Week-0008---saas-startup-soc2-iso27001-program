## 2. What was tabled

Five documents, all in final draft, all circulated on 2027-02-19.

**The written assertion**, in the form at `templates/managements-assertion-template.md`: that the
description presents the system that was designed and implemented throughout the period in accordance with
the description criteria; that the controls stated in it were **suitably designed**; and that they
**operated effectively** throughout the period to provide reasonable assurance that the applicable trust
services criteria were achieved. **DC section 200** and **TSP section 100** named on the face of it, the
carve-out of both subservice organisations stated, and the eleven complementary user entity controls
identified.

**The description of the system**, Section III, including the **DC9** disclosure of six relevant changes and
the two DC4 disclosures.

**Section IV in draft**, with the **nine test exceptions**, their populations, their rates and the service
auditor's evaluation of each.

**The communication to those charged with governance of 2027-02-10** — **GOV-34** — carrying the
near-modification on exception 5 and the reasoning on both sides of it.

**The representation letter** under **O12**, for noting rather than for approval: it is addressed to
Ashcombe &amp; Doyle LLP, it is management's statement to the practitioner, and **no user of the report ever
sees it.**

## 3. The two candidates for not signing

**The question put to the board was not whether the assertion was well drafted. It was whether management
could make it.** Karim Haddad tabled two of the nine exceptions as candidates for not signing, on Rahul
Bhargava's analysis and with the counter-argument attached to each.

**Exception 5 — `CNB-C-126` and `CNB-C-127`, P4.2, 68 of 184 nights, 37.0%.** The ground for not signing was
that P4.2's support in `eu-central-1` for the 119 nights before `CNB-C-149` existed was those two controls
and nothing else, and that the sixty-eight sit inside those hundred and nineteen. **An officer asserting
operating effectiveness has to be able to say what was operating.**

**Exception 1 — `CNB-C-098`, A1.2, 1 of 6, 16.7%.** The ground was narrower and arithmetic: **a monthly
control with a population of six cannot absorb a miss**, the August occurrence is unrecoverable, it was not
re-performed and back-dated, and the assertion is about the period rather than about the intention.

**The board considered both and resolved that the assertion could be made.** For exception 5, on the
service auditor's evaluation as communicated on 2027-02-10, the confinement of the failure to one rule in
one region, its detection inside the period and the independently verified correction — and on the
distinction the assertion turns on, that it concerns whether the **criteria were achieved** rather than
whether every occurrence of every control was clean. For exception 1, on the four further controls serving
A1.2 across the window.

**Lorraine Kessler asked that the consideration be minuted whichever way it went**, and it is. **DEC-902**,
2027-02-24. **A board minute recording that the question was asked is worth more than a signature recorded
without one**, and it is the only evidence that the alternative was live.

**The alternative was live, and the board was told what it meant.** **Had management concluded it could not
sign, the engagement would have ended there and no report would exist.** There is no version of an
assertion-based examination in which a practitioner opines on an assertion the responsible party has
declined to make — and no version in which the practitioner writes one instead.

## 5. What this meeting did not do

**It did not approve the report.** The report is Ashcombe &amp; Doyle's, was issued two days later on
2027-02-26, and no board resolution reaches Section I or Section IV.

**It did not know the opinion.** The engagement team's evaluation had been communicated on 2027-02-10 and
its expectation stated; the report had not been issued and the opinion had not been expressed. **The board
approved an assertion without knowing what would be said about it**, which is the ordinary condition of an
assertion-based engagement and the reason §3 was a real question rather than a ratification.

**It did not close anything.** Sixteen corrective actions were open on this date and sixteen remain open at
2027-03-11. No closure was proposed at this meeting and none would have been in order: `CNB-C-148` refuses
closure until the effectiveness check is complete, and an effectiveness check is a control occurrence rather
than a board resolution.

**And it did not characterise the assertion as an achievement.** The minute records a decision to make a
statement about a period, taken with two reasons not to on the table, and nothing further.
