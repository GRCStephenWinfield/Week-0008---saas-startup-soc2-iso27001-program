## Context

**On 2027-01-28, at the third fieldwork status meeting, the engagement partner raised that the engagement
team was considering whether exception 5 required a modification of the opinion in respect of P4.2.** The
RT-02 deletion job had addressed an emptied relation in `eu-central-1` for sixty-eight consecutive nights,
completing successfully and deleting nothing, on geolocation captured from the individual at clock-in.

`08.11` §5 recorded the question as open and **DEC-815** refused to predict the answer, on ADR-0015's rule
that nothing is published until it has been proved reachable. The report was issued on **2027-02-26** with
an **unmodified opinion** and the exception disclosed in Section IV with the service auditor's evaluation
beside it. The service auditor's reasoning was communicated to those charged with governance on
**2027-02-10** — **GOV-34** — and appears in Section IV as published.

**What had to be decided was what Phase 09 would carry.** The outcome is a fact and would have been reported
whatever was decided. The reasoning is a different question: it is another party's, it is unflattering in
places, and nothing obliged CloudNimbus to reproduce it in its own programme record.

## Decision

**The service auditor's reasoning is published in full at `09.05` §3 — both the facts supporting the
conclusion and the three facts pointing the other way — attributed as the service auditor's, with management
neither endorsing nor contesting it. Both denominators are published with their arithmetic, and the chapter
states which one this programme thinks is honest. And the outcome is recorded as close. DEC-906, taken by
Karim Haddad on 2027-02-27.**

Four limbs, and each was separable from the others.

**The reasoning is published against the outcome rather than after it.** A chapter that opened with an
unmodified opinion and then explained it would be an argument for a conclusion already stated. `09.05`
states what was raised, then the two rates, then the case both ways, then the conclusion.

**The three facts against are printed at the same weight as the facts for.** That the failure ran for 37% of
the period; that no control in the library would have detected it, the detection being incidental to an
evidence request; and that `CNB-C-126` and `CNB-C-127` were the whole of P4.2's support in the affected
region for the 119 nights before `CNB-C-149` existed, with the sixty-eight inside them.

**Both denominators are published.** 68 of 184 = 37.0% and 68 of 4,416 = 1.5%. **A programme that named
only the number supporting its own discomfort would be choosing a denominator too**, so both appear with
their arithmetic and the choice is argued rather than made silently.

**And the outcome is recorded as close.** **A different engagement team, on the same facts, could have
reached the other answer and would not have been wrong to.**

## Consequences

**The chapter teaches something rather than reporting something.** A portfolio that showed the outcome and
not the reasoning would teach a reader that unmodified opinions happen — which is not a lesson, and is the
specific misreading `09.04` §3 exists to prevent. What `09.05` shows instead is the shape of the judgement:
that the distance between an unmodified opinion and a qualified one, in this engagement, was **one judgement
about one denominator**, and that nothing in the facts changed when the judgement landed.

**It removes the entity's ability to present the outcome as earned.** That is the cost and it was accepted
knowingly. A close-out chapter reading *the opinion was unmodified* invites the reading that the control
environment deserved it; a chapter reading *it was close, and here is what was said on the other side*
does not. **An account of this programme that presented the outcome as earned rather than reasoned would
have stopped being useful**, and this is the one place where the difference is visible.

**It constrains what CloudNimbus may say about the reasoning elsewhere.** The reproduction is bounded by two
documents — Section IV as published and GOV-34 — and by nothing else. `logs/evidence-index.md` records that
there is **no artefact for the engagement team's own reasoning beyond those two**, because the service
auditor's working papers are the service auditor's. **A programme holding a document that set out what its
auditor concluded, sourced from anywhere other than the auditor, would be holding a document somebody had
written on the auditor's behalf.**

**And it closes the asymmetry Phase 08 recorded rather than carrying it forward.** `08.11` §5 noted that the
engagement team's stated concerns at the January status meeting were not minuted, because CloudNimbus's
minute of a status meeting is CloudNimbus's record of what CloudNimbus said. **GOV-34 of 2027-02-10 supplied
what Phase 08 said was missing**: the engagement team put both sides of its evaluation on the record itself,
in its own words, and `09.05` §3 reproduces them from that record and from Section IV as published. **The
five facts for and the three against are the service auditor's throughout, and neither set is management's
case** — which is why the chapter counts facts rather than paragraphs, and why a count of them is not a
weight.

## Alternatives considered and rejected

**Report the outcome alone** — rejected. It was the easiest option, it required no attribution work, and it
would have produced a close-out in which the most consequential judgement of the engagement appeared as a
one-line result. **The outcome is the least transferable part of this story**; another organisation cannot
use CloudNimbus's unmodified opinion and can use the shape of the question that nearly modified it.

**Publish the reasoning and characterise it as vindication** — rejected, and it is the version this
programme would have written in its first phase. The facts supporting the conclusion are real: the failure
was confined to one rule in one region, detected inside the period, corrected in four days, independently
verified, and a control was admitted on its evidence. **Presenting them as a defence rather than as one side
of an evaluation would have re-used an auditor's reasoning as a testimonial**, which is worse than not
publishing it.

**Publish the reasoning without recording that it was close** — rejected. The closeness is the finding.
Without it the chapter reads as a set of countervailing facts that obviously outweighed a deviation, and
every organisation reading it would conclude that a well-managed retention failure carries no risk to an
opinion. **It carried a real one for four weeks, and the record has to say so.**

**Ask Ashcombe &amp; Doyle to review or approve `09.05`** — rejected, and it was not available in any case.
The chapter is CloudNimbus's programme record, not a communication from the practitioner, and a service
auditor asked to review an entity's account of the auditor's own reasoning would be being asked to lend it
standing it does not have. **Section IV and GOV-34 already carry the reasoning in the practitioner's own
words**, and those two documents are the source and the limit.

**Wait for the 2027 examination before publishing** — rejected. Nothing about the next window changes what
happened in this one, and a programme that deferred an uncomfortable disclosure to a period in which it
might look smaller would be doing what `09.05` §2 refuses to do with a denominator.
