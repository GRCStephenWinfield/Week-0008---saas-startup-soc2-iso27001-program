**DEC-911 and DEC-912 are one decision split in two, and the split is the substance.** Scoring OBJ-03 as
missed and referring the measure elsewhere are separable acts, and a programme that took them together would
have produced the outcome both were designed to prevent: a corrected measure applied in the document that
grades the objective. The scoring belongs to the close-out because the close-out is where objectives are
scored. **The measure belongs to the body that sets objectives, which meets annually under `CNB-C-016` and
does not meet on 2027-03-11.**

**DEC-913 is the decision this phase would be judged on if a reader could only check one.** Sixteen
corrective actions are open at the close of a programme whose deliverables are both complete, and three of
them have sixteen weeks, a verified correction and a corrected path behind them. Each of the three is an
argument that something worked. **None of them is the effectiveness check `CNB-C-148` requires**, and the
convenient alternative was to close them with a caveat — which is a form of words that admits the closure
was taken for a reason other than the check.

**DEC-908 was taken in the week it was hardest to take.** The report existed, 118 enterprise-tier customers
had it, and the request that arrived was for a link on the trust centre. **A restricted-use report is not a
general-use document**, no phase of this programme has ever committed to making it generally available, and
the commitment in SC-10 is to provide it under non-disclosure on request. The decision cost a fortnight of
sales friction and is the only thing standing between the report and a population of readers the restriction
was written to exclude.

**DEC-902 records a consideration rather than an act, and it is in the log for that reason.** Management
signed an assertion of operating effectiveness for a period containing nine test exceptions. Two of the nine
were put to the board as candidates for not signing — exception 5, because P4.2's support in the affected
region was thin for 37% of the period, and exception 1, because a monthly control with a population of six
cannot absorb a miss. **Had management concluded it could not sign, the engagement would have ended there
and no report would exist.** A board minute recording that the question was asked is worth more than a
signature recorded without one.

**DEC-904 is the smallest decision in the log and the easiest to get wrong.** SC-01 always carried the
announced-maintenance exclusion; `02.12` v1.0 omitted it and v1.1 restored it under DEC-613 in September.
That is a correction to the description of a commitment, not a change to the system, and filing it under DC9
would have told a reader the commitment changed mid-period when it did not. **The distinction DC9 draws in
time is drawn here in kind**, and both are the same discipline.
