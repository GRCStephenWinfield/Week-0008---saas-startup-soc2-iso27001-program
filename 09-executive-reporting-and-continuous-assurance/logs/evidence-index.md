that are unusual for a closing index: a reconciliation that grades a forecast the programme published about
itself, and a register of sixteen corrective actions none of which is closed.** Neither is flattering and
both are the point.

## Three of these units, and what each of them holds

**EV-914 is a unit whose only purpose is to let somebody grade the programme.** It holds the forecast the
programme published about itself in April 2026, the position it actually reached in December 2026, and the
difference on every one of thirty-eight rows — including the six rows where the ratings differ and no band
moved, which no summary the register has ever produced would show. It was generated by the same harness that
refused to publish the forecast until the arithmetic had been re-derived from the register. **A programme
that builds a tool to prove its forecast reachable and does not point the same tool at the outcome has built
half a tool**, and the eleven divergences in this unit are what the second half found.

**EV-920 holds sixteen open records and no closed one.** That is not an omission and it is not a backlog. It
is `CNB-C-148`'s closure condition applied at the moment it was most inconvenient: an effectiveness check is
a control occurrence with a result, twelve of the sixteen attach to controls whose next occurrence falls
after this vantage, and four belong to Northgate's verification at the 2027-11 surveillance audit. **The
unit also holds three records annotated with the argument for closing them**, which is the honest form:
somebody made the case, the case was not the check, and the record shows both.

**EV-909 holds a determination on every one of 155 rows where a careless record would hold a timestamp.**
The despatch and the acknowledgement say the delivery happened. The determination says which class of
intended user the recipient falls in and why, and it is the only field that shows the restricted-use
paragraph was applied rather than printed. **A distribution record with 155 timestamps and no determinations
is a record of an operation**, and the operation was never the thing that had to be evidenced.

## What there is no artefact for

**There is no operating record for the 2027 observation window.** It opened 2027-01-01, is ten weeks old on
this vantage, and holds no complete population for any monthly, quarterly or annual control. **No rate is
stateable and none is stated**, for the same reason 06.12 and 07.12 refused one twice: a denominator that is
still accruing produces a number that will be wrong by the time anybody reads it.

**There is no artefact for the first surveillance audit**, which falls within twelve months of the
certification decision of 2027-01-20 and is scheduled for **2027-11** under O11. It has not happened.

**There is no artefact for `CNB-C-150` operating**, and there will be none until the 2028 audit programme is
set — `IS-36`.

**There is no artefact for any corrective action's effectiveness check**, because none has been performed —
which is the whole content of EV-920 and the reason nothing closed.

**There is no artefact recording the engagement team's own reasoning beyond what EV-905 and EV-906 carry.**
The service auditor's working papers are the service auditor's. What CloudNimbus holds is the report as
issued and the communication to those charged with governance, and 09.05 reproduces the reasoning from those
two documents and from nothing else. **A programme holding a document that set out what its auditor
concluded, sourced from anywhere other than the auditor, would be holding a document somebody had written on
the auditor's behalf.**

**And there is no artefact for the two bridge letters that cannot yet be signed.** Their gap periods end
after any date on which management could state its knowledge, the requests are open on EV-912, and a letter
written in advance of the period it covers is a representation nobody could have made.
