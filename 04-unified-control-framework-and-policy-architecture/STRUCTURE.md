Every workbook is generated by parsing the narrative. The build **derives the 112/21/15 split from the
mapping columns rather than reading it from a declaration**, re-counts every family, type and cadence, and
checks that all 61 criteria and all 91 necessary Annex A controls are reached. A workbook cannot disagree
with the prose, and the prose cannot disagree with its own arithmetic.
