## 1. Matter

Declaration that the information security management system is operational — milestone **MS-09**,
2026-06-15 — and reissue of the Statement of Applicability at the same date.

## 2. What "operational" means here, and what it does not

It means the ISMS required by clauses 4 to 10 exists and is running: a scope, a policy, objectives, a risk
assessment and treatment process with results, a Statement of Applicability, documented information under
clause 7.5, controls with owners and cadences, and the machinery of clauses 9 and 10 defined and scheduled.

**It does not mean the controls have operated.** Sixteen days separate this declaration from the opening of
the observation window, and a control that has run once has an operating history of one. The declaration is
about the management system's existence, not about its effectiveness, and the two are assessed by different
things — clause 9 assesses the second, over time.

## 3. The Statement of Applicability reissue

Version 1.0 was approved on 2026-04-24 with **62 implemented, 21 partially implemented and 8 planned**
against 91 necessary controls. Clause 6.1.3 d) requires the Statement to say whether the necessary controls
are implemented, and a build running to June meant that first issue showed a build in progress.

The reissue records the position at the operational date. **DEC-412** requires the reissue to be dated,
version-controlled and retained alongside v1.0 rather than replacing it: a Statement of Applicability whose
history has been overwritten cannot show what was true when a decision was taken.

## 4. R-37, and what it demonstrated

The register grew for the first time on 2026-05-22, on evidence from penetration testing. **R-37** entered
at 4 × 5 = 20, High; it was remediated on 2026-05-29, retested clean on 2026-06-11, and re-rated at this
review to 2 × 5 = 10, Moderate.

Phase 03 provided for exactly this at **DEC-306** — the register was left open to additions on evidence,
and its published forecast assumed two would arrive. The committee recorded the arrival of the first as
evidence that the provision was not decorative.

**The other thirty-six were confirmed without change**, because a treatment implemented in June has no
operating history in June. **The register stands at 37 entries: 7 High · 18 Moderate · 12 Low.**
