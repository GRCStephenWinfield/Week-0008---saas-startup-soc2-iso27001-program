## 1. What Phase 04 fixed

Phase 01 established who decides and against what. Phase 02 established what is in. Phase 03 established
what could go wrong and which controls are necessary. **Phase 04 established what CloudNimbus actually
does, who does it, how often, under what authority, and what it leaves behind.**

It published the **unified control library at v1.0 on 2026-06-05** (MS-07, DEC-410): **148 controls,
`CNB-C-001` to `CNB-C-148`**, keyed to control identifiers rather than to framework references, with the
**112 dual, 21 SOC 2-only and 15 ISO-only** split derived from the Criteria and Annex A columns rather than
declared.

It approved the **nineteen-policy suite on 2026-06-12** (MS-08, DEC-411), POL-01 being the clause 5.2
policy approved by the Chief Executive Officer as top management and the other eighteen approved by the
accountable executive for their subject matter. Every control cites exactly one policy and every policy is
cited by at least one control, because **a control with no policy behind it is a habit, and a policy with
no control under it is a wish.**

It declared **twenty-four evidence classes, EC-01 to EC-24**, before the controls were built, each stating
what the artefact is, where it is stored, how long it is kept, and what one unit of it looks like. The last
is the ML-1 corrective: a control that worked and left nothing a sampler could use.

It registered **thirty-seven items of documented information** under clause 7.5 — fourteen required by the
standard, twenty-three determined necessary — each with an owner, a location, a version-control route and a
retention period, and each changed only by an approval that precedes the change.

And it stated, before the window opened, that **twelve of the twenty-two annual controls fall inside it and
ten do not, and that the ten are being re-scheduled so that they do** (DEC-409, ADR-0019), publishing the
derivation control by control against the assurance calendar rather than asserting the total. One of the
ten is `CNB-C-031`, the single control serving CC5.3, which without the re-scheduling would have had no
occurrence in the period at all. Re-performing an annual control inside an observation window so that it
has a population is legitimate to do and dishonest to hide. **MS-09**
followed on 2026-06-15: the **ISMS was declared operational and the Statement of Applicability reissued**
under DEC-412.

| ADR-0019 | Ten annual controls re-scheduled into the window, and disclosed | 2026-06-01 | Karim Haddad |
| ADR-0020 | No control is owned by a team | 2026-05-15 | Karim Haddad |

**ADR-0018 is the phase's centre of gravity**, and it is a lesson institutionalised rather than a design
preference — the same shape as ADR-0015 in Phase 03. The programme has already been told, by its own
predecessor auditor, that a working control can leave unusable evidence.

**ADR-0019 is the one a reader should test the programme against.** It makes CloudNimbus look worse in the
short term — ten controls whose natural rhythm does not reach the window, nearly half the annual
population, one of them the only control serving a criterion — and better in the only term that matters.

## 4. The register at this vantage: 37 entries

Phase 03 baselined **36 risks — 7 High, 17 Moderate, 12 Low** — and recorded at **DEC-306** that the
register was not closed to additions on evidence. The first addition arrived six weeks later. **R-37 was
admitted on 2026-05-22** on evidence from the penetration testing that completed that day (MS-06), entering
at **4 × 5 = 20, High**; it was remediated on 2026-05-29, retested clean on 2026-06-11, and **re-rated at
the ISMS operational review to 2 × 5 = 10, Moderate**. The substance of the finding is Phase 05's.

**The register therefore reads 37 entries: 7 High · 18 Moderate · 12 Low.** 36 = 7 + 17 + 12; R-37 enters
High, making 8 + 17 + 12 = 37; the re-rating moves it to Moderate, making 7 + 18 + 12 = 37.

**The other 36 entries have not been re-rated, and DEC-408 records that as a decision rather than an
oversight.** Ratings move at review points, not continuously. The controls published on 2026-06-05 are
expected to reduce likelihood across much of the register; none of that reduction is recorded yet. A
register that moved every time a control was implemented would be scored on intention.

**Gap 4 has the sharpest consequence.** A criterion served by a single control has no redundancy: if that
control has a test exception, the criterion is unsupported. The concentration is disclosed rather than
diluted, because the alternative — writing a second control where no second mechanism exists — is
documentation, not control design.

## 7. What could still go wrong

**A published library is not an operating library.** Every row in the four control tables is a statement
written by the people who built the mechanism, and none has been tested by anybody independent. The window
opens tomorrow, and the distance between "designed and implemented" and "operated on each occasion across
six months" is the distance between a Type I and a Type II.

**The quarterly and semi-annual controls are the exposure.** Twenty-seven quarterly controls contribute two
occurrences each and five semi-annual controls one. A single late occurrence is a deviation rate of 50% or
100%, and the slip that produces it — a review completed a week late because the reviewer was on leave — is
precisely the failure TH-14 describes, which generates more of the register than any attacker-driven
threat.

**The ten re-scheduled annual controls must be disclosed where the reader will look.** ADR-0019 is a
programme record; the description of the system is what a user entity reads. The disclosure belongs under
**DC5** — the applicable trust services criteria and the related controls, which this phase carries — and
under **DC9**, relevant changes during the period, which Phase 09 carries. It is not a DC4 matter; DC4 is
about incidents. If the disclosure stops at the decision record, an honest decision will have been
communicated to nobody — worse than not identifying the issue, because it is a position taken knowingly.

**The evidence architecture is a design and not yet a habit.** What decides whether the store holds
sampleable evidence in January is whether the exports run for twenty-six weeks and whether the people
operating the controls produce the unit the class describes. **ML-1 was not a failure of design; it was a
failure of what the design turned into once it met a busy quarter.**
