## 1. What the engagement is, stated once and precisely

**Ashcombe &amp; Doyle LLP performed fieldwork from 2027-01-12 to 2027-02-05 — nineteen working days.**
Engagement partner **Meredith Vance, CPA**; manager **Dev Ramanathan**; three staff. This chapter is written
on the last of those nineteen days.

The engagement is an **examination** conducted under the AICPA attestation standards — **AT-C section 105**,
which carries the concepts common to all attestation engagements, and **AT-C section 205**, which since
**SSAE No. 21** is titled *Assertion-Based Examination Engagements*. In it the practitioner obtains
**reasonable assurance** about whether management's description of the system is presented in accordance
with the description criteria, and whether the controls stated in that description were **suitably
designed** and **operated effectively throughout the period** to provide reasonable assurance that the
applicable trust services criteria were achieved.

**The criteria are TSP section 100 — the 2017 trust services criteria with revised points of focus, 2022 —
and DC section 200, the 2018 description criteria, DC1 to DC9.** Sixty-one criteria are in scope, because
management selected all five categories at scoping and there is no mechanism in the trust services criteria
for setting one aside.

**The practitioner does not design the controls, does not select them, and does not test the complementary
user entity controls.** All three limits matter and each is a different kind of limit. Design and selection
are management's, and Ashcombe &amp; Doyle's independence under **AICPA ET section 1.295** depends on their
staying management's — which is why Solstice Trust Advisors did the readiness work and Ashcombe &amp; Doyle
did not. The eleven CUECs are controls of the **user entity**, performed by the customer, and it is the
customer's own auditor who would test them; the description states what each requires and for which
criteria it is necessary, which is what DC6 asks for, and it stops there. **Nobody in this engagement tested
whether 640 employers reconcile their exports.**

**And nothing in the carved-out subservice organisations was tested either.** AWS's and Halcyon Identity's
controls are excluded from the description under the carve-out method, with fourteen complementary
subservice organisation controls stated instead. What was tested is CloudNimbus's configuration of the
services those organisations provide, which is a different subject and the one that belongs in Section IV.

## 2. How nineteen days were spent

| Week | Dates | Activity |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 2027-01-12 to 01-15 | Description walkthrough against DC1 to DC9; scope confirmation; the ten re-scheduled annual controls and the two mid-window library changes discussed before any selection was made |
| 2 | 2027-01-18 to 01-22 | Common Criteria testing — CC1 to CC5 governance and the CC6 access family; the first two exceptions raised |
| 3 | 2027-01-25 to 01-29 | CC6 to CC9, A1, PI1; the 2027-01-28 status meeting at which the near-modification was raised |
| 4 | 2027-02-01 to 02-05 | C1, P, the Stage 2 collision evaluated as contradictory evidence, and the closing status meeting |

**Four status meetings were held, one in each week of fieldwork** — **2027-01-15, 01-22, 01-28 and 02-05**;
the third fell on the Thursday and the other three on the Friday, and the record carries the dates rather
than a cadence the dates do not support. Every exception was raised at the meeting following the testing
that produced it rather than accumulated to the end. That is the arrangement
CloudNimbus asked for at the **CAL-05** service-auditor checkpoint that has run monthly since 2026-04, and
the reason is not comfort. **An exception raised in week two can be understood while the person who
operated the control is at their desk**; the same exception raised at a closing meeting is answered by
whoever is in the room.

The description walkthrough in week one is the part most easily under-described. **Ashcombe &amp; Doyle did
not begin by testing controls.** They began with the description — the system boundary, the components, the
commitments, the criteria and the controls management asserts meet them — because a control tested against
a description that is not fairly presented is a test of nothing. Two disclosures were confirmed in that
week and both had been drafted months earlier: **the re-scheduling of ten annual controls into the window
under ADR-0019**, and **SC-01's maintenance-window exclusion**, corrected at source in September under
DEC-613. Neither was discovered in fieldwork. **A disclosure the entity has to explain in January is a
worse disclosure than the same fact written down in June**, and 04.11 §4.4 said so before the window
opened.

## 3. What was given and what was asked for

The 427-item request list and the 1,847 artefacts it produced belong to 08.09. Two facts about the exchange
belong here, because they are about the examination rather than about the store.

**Every population was stated by CloudNimbus and every selection was made by Ashcombe &amp; Doyle.** The
entity produced the population — 312 changes, 10,336 alerts, 40 provisioning requests, 6 restore tests, 24
EC-01 access review units and the 47 revocations arising from them — and the engagement team drew from it. An entity that produces a *sample* has produced its own
evidence about its own controls, and the distinction is the difference between a test and a demonstration.

**Two matters were volunteered before they were asked for.** The first is the position on subservice
organisation coverage that `IS-31` records — that **two months of the window are reached by no artefact
from either AWS or Halcyon Identity**, and that Halcyon Identity's report period ended the day before the
window opened. 07.11 recorded the plan to state it in advance rather than have it discovered, and it was
stated on the second day. The second is the Stage 2 major nonconformity, which is 08.12's subject.

**Volunteering is not a virtue and it is not generosity.** Both facts were discoverable — one from two
bridge letters and a subtraction, the other from a certificate dated inside the fieldwork period — and an
entity that waits to be asked about a discoverable fact has spent the two things it cannot replace: the
chance to state the fact in its own terms, and the presumption that it knew. What volunteering buys is
narrow and worth naming precisely: it moves the conversation from *why did you not tell us* to *what do you
make of it*, and only the second of those is a conversation about the evidence.

### 3.1 Two mid-window changes to the library, and how each was tested

The control library was 148 rows when the window opened and 149 when it closed. Two things changed inside
the period and each was tested as what it was.

**`CNB-C-096` was amended on 2026-09-15 under DEC-609**, with the write probe deployed on 2026-09-19. The
control operated under the original statement from 2026-07-01 to 2026-09-18 and under the amended statement
from 2026-09-19, and it was tested against **both**. That is the only defensible treatment: a control tested
across a period against the statement it acquired part-way through would be tested against a description
that was not in force for most of the occurrences selected. Exception 2 arises under the original statement,
because that is the statement that was in force on 2026-09-08.

**`CNB-C-149` was admitted on 2026-10-28 under DEC-706**, and its population is the 65 nights it existed.
Nothing about the admission reaches the 119 nights before it, and ADR-0031 said so on the day it was taken
rather than being asked in January. A control admitted mid-window is a **relevant change during the period**
and belongs under **DC9** in the description, which Phase 09 carries; it is not a DC5 cadence question and
it is not a DC4 incident disclosure.

## 4. Populations, samples, and the ten with a population of one

| Control | Cadence | Window population | Sample |
|---|---|---|---|
| `CNB-C-079` change peer review | Continuous | **312** merged changes | 40 |
| `CNB-C-087` deployment reconciliation | Daily | **184** daily runs | 25 |
| `CNB-C-067` alert triage | Continuous | **10,336** alerts | 45 |
| `CNB-C-126`, `CNB-C-127` scheduled deletion | Daily | **184 nights × 8 rules × 3 regions** | 60 job-nights |
| `CNB-C-108` reconciliation exception clearance | Weekly | **58** exceptions | **58 — tested in full** |
| `CNB-C-129` data subject request assistance | Monthly | **58** requests | **58 — tested in full** |
| `CNB-C-040` quarterly entitlement certification | Quarterly | **24** EC-01 units — 12 systems × 2 quarters — and the **47** revocations arising | **24 and 47 — tested in full** |
| `CNB-C-092` Tier 1 assurance refresh | Quarterly | **24** readings | **24 — tested in full** |
| `CNB-C-082` emergency change approval | Monthly | **15** emergency changes, **6** log reviews | **15 and 6 — tested in full** |
| `CNB-C-037` access provisioning | Continuous | **40** | **40 — tested in full** |
| `CNB-C-098` restore test | Monthly | **6** | **6 — tested in full** |
| `CNB-C-100` disaster recovery exercise | Annual | **1** | **1** |
| `CNB-C-016` annual objectives setting | Annual | **1** | **1** |
| `CNB-C-031` annual procedure inventory | Annual | **1** | **1** |

**`CNB-C-040`'s row states two populations because the control has two limbs and they are counted in
different units.** `04.12` declares EC-01's sampling unit as *one system for one quarter*; twelve systems
across two quarterly certifications is **24 units**, and that is the population of the certification limb.
The revocations arising are not EC-01 units and are counted as themselves — **47**. Stating the first as
*two certifications* would describe the calendar rather than the declared unit, and folding the second into
it would hide the limb exception 9 is recorded against. **A population carries its derivation**, which is the
evidence-request template's own rule and the reason the cell shows the multiplication.

**The rule Ashcombe &amp; Doyle applied is visible in the table: a population at or below sixty was tested in
full, and above it a sample was drawn.** That is why several of the deviation rates in 08.11 are rates over
the whole population rather than over a selection, and it makes those rates stronger rather than weaker.

**The ten controls ADR-0019 re-scheduled into the window each have a population of one, and every one was
tested in full**, because a population of one cannot be sampled. `CNB-C-008`, `CNB-C-010`, `CNB-C-016`,
`CNB-C-026`, `CNB-C-031`, `CNB-C-099`, `CNB-C-116`, `CNB-C-120`, `CNB-C-136` and `CNB-C-137` are the ten,
and each was disclosed in the description with its cadence, its previous operation, its re-scheduled
operation and the reason — which is the disclosure 04.11 §4.4 committed the programme to before anybody
knew whether it would be convenient.

**At a population of one, a single deviation is a deviation rate of one hundred per cent.** That is exactly
what happened to **`CNB-C-136`**, whose sole window occurrence — the re-scheduled 2026-08 re-derivation of
ICT continuity requirements from the business impact analysis — did not take place. It is `D-06-05`. The
occurrence was performed on **2026-12-08** under `CA-06-04`, four months after it was due, and that is a
clause 10.2 correction rather than a recovery: **an annual control's occurrence is an event on a date, and
performing it in December does not make it have happened in August.**

**`CNB-C-136` is ISO-only. It cites no trust services criterion, so it is a nonconformity and not a test
exception**, and it is the clearest illustration the engagement produced that the two frameworks do not
partition the same facts the same way. The same missed month is a clause 10.2 matter for Northgate, an
`AOC-03` area of concern at Stage 1, and **nothing at all** for Ashcombe &amp; Doyle, who cannot record a
deviation against a control that appears in no Section IV. 08.12 owns that argument in full.

**And `CNB-C-031` is the row a reader should test the re-scheduling against.** It is the single control
serving **CC5.3**. On its natural anniversary of June 2027 it would not have operated inside the window at
all, and CC5.3 — a criterion served by one control — would have had no testable occurrence. It was
performed on **2026-09-17**, in full, with 147 procedures named, 143 confirmed and 4 not, the four
re-assigned and reviewed by 2026-09-25 before the confirmation was signed. **The re-scheduling is the
difference between CC5.3 being supported by one occurrence and CC5.3 being supported by none**, and the
four unconfirmed procedures are the evidence that the confirmation reached the estate rather than the list.

**GOV-20 made the small-population argument in August**, when a month's access provisioning produced three
requests and the record said plainly that three is not a population. **The window closed with forty, and
forty was still small enough to test in full** — which is the argument's real conclusion and the less
comfortable half of it. A population large enough to sample is not a population large enough to be
reassuring, and a population small enough to test exhaustively leaves nowhere for a deviation to average
itself away. **Two deviations in forty is 5.0%, and it is 5.0% of everything that happened.**

The same arithmetic runs the other way and is worth stating so that the small-population disclosure does not
read as an excuse. **`CNB-C-087` ran 184 times with 0 unmatched deployments**, and a sample of 25 drawn from
184 supports a much weaker claim about the other 159 than 40 of 40 supports about 40. **A large population
sampled is not better evidence than a small population tested in full; it is evidence about a different
thing.** The first says something about a system's behaviour and accepts sampling risk to say it. The second
says everything there is to say about a period and nothing at all about any other. Neither is preferable in
the abstract, and a description that presented the 184 as reassuring and the 6 as a limitation would have
had it backwards.

**`CNB-C-031` is the reason 04.11 §4.3 exists**, and it is the sharpest case in the table. It has a
population of one and it is **the whole of the evidence about CC5.3**, because CC5.3 is served by that
control alone. A1.3 is better placed by comparison — `CNB-C-100`'s exercise of 2026-08-19 and `CNB-C-101`'s
of 2026-11-12 both serve it, and two occurrences from two separately-owned mechanisms is a different
proposition from one. **A single-control criterion with a low-frequency control is the sharpest exposure a
control library can create**, 04.03 §5 said so in June, and no amount of careful sampling in January could
have altered it. The only two things the programme could do — re-schedule the occurrence into the window and
disclose that it had done so — were both done before the window opened.

## 5. What fieldwork closing does not mean

Fieldwork closed on **2027-02-05**. Nine test exceptions are known and are set out in 08.11. Six of them
were recorded by CloudNimbus in Phase 06 or Phase 07 before fieldwork opened, and every one of the nine was
found by CloudNimbus before it was found by the service auditor.

**None of that is a conclusion, and this chapter does not offer one.** The evaluation of whether the
deviations mean the applicable trust services criteria were achieved is the service auditor's work, it is
performed after fieldwork rather than during it, and **management does not know its outcome on this
vantage.** ADR-0015's rule — no forecast published until it has been proved reachable — has been applied to
a register in Phase 03 and to an opinion in Phases 06, 07 and now here. The report is **planned** for issue
on **2027-02-26**.

**Three things remain outstanding on CloudNimbus's side and each is named rather than left as a formality.**
Management's written assertion and the representation letter under **O12** are owed at the conclusion of the
examination and are Phase 09's. The description, including the **DC9** disclosure of relevant changes during
the period — `CNB-C-149`'s admission in October chief among them — is completed in Phase 09. And the two
final subservice organisation bridge letters, requested on 2026-12-15 and escalated on 2027-01-08, are what
`IS-31` and `CA-07-06` describe: **management-issued, unaudited statements covering a gap, which cover a gap
and do not close one.**
