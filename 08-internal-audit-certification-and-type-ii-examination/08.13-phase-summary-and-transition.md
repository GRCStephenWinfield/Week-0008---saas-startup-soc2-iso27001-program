## 1. What Phase 08 established

Phase 07 closed on a control that did everything asked of it and could not see what it was being asked
about. **Phase 08 closes on a programme that wrote down its own worst finding eleven months before an
auditor raised it, and deferred it twice** — the audit programme document was written at chartering in
January 2026 and `MAJ-01` was raised on 2026-12-02, which is the clock that matters here.

Five positions, each owned and argued elsewhere, none restated here.

| Position | Where it is owned |
|---|---|
| **The clause 9.2 internal audit covered the 93 Annex A controls and did not cover clauses 4 to 10 or `eu-central-1`.** Camberwell's report stated the limitation at §1.3 and recommended it be reconsidered before the certification audit; it was read, agreed, minuted at the management review and deferred | [08.01 §5](08.01-the-clause-9-2-internal-audit.md); DEC-801, DEC-802 |
| **`MAJ-01` is a capability finding and was accepted at the closing meeting without contest.** Clause 9.2 is one of the mechanisms by which a management system checks itself, and the same failure engages A.5.22 — recorded as one nonconformity, not two | [08.05 §4 and §6](08.05-stage-2-and-the-major-nonconformity.md); DEC-806; ADR-0037 |
| **The library got ahead of the practice and nothing checked that they still agreed.** The audit programme document was written in January 2026 against the Statement of Applicability; `CNB-C-146` was written in June and describes a programme nobody brought the document up to | [08.07 §3](08.07-correction-corrective-action-and-the-certificate.md) |
| **The register closed at 0 High · 17 Moderate · 21 Low**, nineteen entries moved at one review, seven held, twelve already-Low unchanged. Nothing closed and nothing removed at any point in the register's life | [08.08 §5](08.08-the-observation-window-closes.md); GOV-32; ADR-0039 |
| **Nine test exceptions, every one found by CloudNimbus before it was found by the service auditor**, and the engagement team raised on 2027-01-28 that it was considering whether exception 5 required a modification | [08.11](08.11-the-nine-test-exceptions.md); DEC-815 |

**The sentence this phase would keep if it could keep one** is 08.03 §5's: *a Stage 1 that raises nothing
about your internal audit is not evidence that your internal audit is adequate; it is evidence that you have
one.* CloudNimbus read a clean Stage 1 as confirmation that deferring the scope question was safe, without
anybody writing that inference down, and ADR-0036 records the misreading rather than the decision because
the misreading is the transferable half.

## 2. The certificate, and what it is not

**ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certificate issued 2027-01-22 by Northgate Certification Services, Ltd., valid to
2030-01-21.** Northgate is accredited by **ANAB** under **ISO/IEC 17021-1** and **ISO/IEC 27006-1:2024**.
**ISO does not issue it and has no view on it.** The certificate states the standard, the scope as the ISMS
scope statement reissued on 2026-10-28, and a three-year validity. The first surveillance audit must be
conducted within twelve months of the **certification decision of 2027-01-20** and is scheduled for
**2027-11**; recertification falls in year three.

**It is not a statement that CloudNimbus is secure. It is not a statement about any control's operating
effectiveness over a period.** That is the other document, and **it has not been issued.**

**And it is not a determination of compliance with any law** — not any privacy statute, not any state law,
not any regulation. Neither deliverable is, and this portfolio has said so in every phase since the first.

## 3. What the phase did not do

**It did not argue the major down.** A version of `MAJ-01` could have been put as a minor: the Annex A
coverage was genuine, the programme was resourced, the auditor was independent. **The organisation did not
put it**, on the reasoning 08.05 §6 sets out, and there is no dissent recorded in this phase because nobody
disagreed. Phases 05 and 07 carry dissents because reasonable people differed; manufacturing one here to
match a pattern would have been the dishonest move.

**It did not treat the four opportunities for improvement from the January correction audit as findings, and
it did not treat Northgate's seven as findings either.** **An opportunity for improvement is not a
nonconformity and carries no obligation.** 08.06 says what CloudNimbus did with them anyway, which is a
different statement from being required to.

**It did not back-date, re-perform or repair anything to improve a denominator.** `CNB-C-098` closed the
window at five of six. `CNB-C-136`'s August occurrence was performed on 2026-12-08 as a clause 10.2
correction and the August deviation stands. `CNB-C-149`'s population is 65 nights of 184. `CNB-C-150` was
admitted on **2027-01-08**, after the window closed, **has no population inside it at all, and is not part
of the Type II examination** — ADR-0040 and DEC-811.

**And it did not compute a window availability average**, for the sixth phase running.

**ADR-0040 is the one a reader should test this phase against.** It records a decision that makes the
programme's own remediation invisible to the examination: a control admitted eight days after the window
closed, answering the phase's largest finding, and deliberately excluded from the population the service
auditor tests. The convenient alternative — dating it inside the window, where the correction would have
counted for something — was available and was refused on ADR-0026's reasoning, which has now been applied
three times to three different temptations.

## 6. The register at close

**38 entries — 0 High · 17 Moderate · 21 Low.** Thirty-six have been on the register since the baseline of
2026-04-10 and two were added on evidence: R-37 on 2026-05-22 and R-38 on 2026-10-23. **Nothing has ever
been closed and nothing has ever been removed.**

Against the Phase 03 forecast of **0 · 16 · 22** the arithmetic at the band level is one entry: High 0
against 0, Moderate 17 against 16, Low 21 against 22. **What that difference is made of runs underneath the
bands, and this chapter does not open it.** The reconciliation is Phase 09's — entry by entry and in both
directions — and a phase that both published the close position and marked it against its own forecast would
be doing two jobs in one paragraph. Previewing the result here would be doing the second job badly and
calling it the first.

## 7. The opinion is not stated

**Nothing in this phase says what the opinion will be.**

Fieldwork closed on 2027-02-05. The nine exceptions are known and their populations are complete. On
2027-01-28 Meredith Vance raised that the engagement team was considering whether exception 5 required a
modification, and management set out the countervailing facts. **The question is open, it is the service
auditor's to answer, and management does not know the answer on this vantage.**

There is no assertion. There is no representation letter. There is no report, no Section I, no bridge
letter and no distribution. **The report is planned for issue on 2027-02-26, and that is a plan.**

**And the lesson Phase 09 has to report without softening it.** A clean Type I in 2025 told CloudNimbus
exactly where it would fail a Type II. ML-1 said the access review evidence would not support a sample and
ML-2 said change-management peer review was enforced by convention. Both controls were rebuilt, both
evidence trails were redesigned, and **eighteen months later ML-1's and ML-2's subject matter produced two
of the nine exceptions anyway.** **Remediating a control is not the same as remediating the evidence trail
it leaves behind — and it is not the same as remediating everything the control's failure mode can reach.**

## 9. What could still go wrong

**`CA-08-01` and `CA-08-02` are open on findings seven and five and a half weeks old** — exception 8 was
found on 2026-12-18 and exception 9 on 2026-12-29, against this vantage of 2027-02-05 — and they are open
against controls whose next occurrences fall outside anything this phase reports.

**`CA-06-02` is open and has now been proved right about itself.** `CNB-C-108`'s two-business-day service
level and weekly Tuesday queue produced a second exception on 2026-12-03, on exactly the day of the week
06.13 §9 predicted three months earlier.

**`IS-35` is the class of failure rather than the instance** — the audit programme document and `CNB-C-146`
diverged and nothing compared them, and nothing in the library yet requires any two documents describing
the same activity to be compared. **`IS-36` is a control with no population until the 2028 programme is
set**, so `CNB-C-150`'s first effectiveness check is a year away and its admission cannot be tested by
anybody before then.

**And `IS-31` is not closed.** Two months of the observation window are reached by no artefact from either
subservice organisation, the final bridge letters were requested on 2026-12-15 and escalated on 2027-01-08,
and what the service auditor makes of that is not something any document in this phase forecasts.
