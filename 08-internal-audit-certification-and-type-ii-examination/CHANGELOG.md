### The four things it deliberately does not do

It does not **state the opinion**. Fieldwork closed on the vantage; the assertion, the representation
letter, the report, Section I, the bridge letter and the distribution are Phase 09's, and the
near-modification raised on 2027-01-28 is reported as **open**.

It does not **round the certificate up**. A certificate is a statement that a management system conforms to
clauses 4 to 10, assessed by audit at the opening of a three-year cycle by an accredited certification body. It is
not a statement that CloudNimbus is secure and it is not a statement about any control's operating
effectiveness over a period.

It does not **soften the major**. There is no dissent recorded against `MAJ-01`, because nobody disagreed,
and inventing disagreement to match the pattern of two earlier phases would have been the dishonest move.

It does not **let outsourcing carry the blame the scope decision earns**. Camberwell Risk Partners audited
what it was asked to audit and said in writing that the ask was too narrow.
