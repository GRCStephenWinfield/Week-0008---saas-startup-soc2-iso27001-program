**DEC-801 and DEC-802 are the two decisions this phase exists to record, and neither of them is a mistake in
the ordinary sense.** Karim Haddad read a scope note recommending that the exclusion of clauses 4 to 10 and
of one region be reconsidered before the certification audit, agreed with it, and scheduled the coverage
into the following year's programme. Elise Fontaine then took the same note at the clause 9.3 management
review under input d) 3) and confirmed the deferral. **Nothing was missed, nothing was hidden, and both
decisions were minuted at the point they were taken.** They are what a major nonconformity looks like when
it is written down in advance by the people it will later be raised against — and 08.01 §5 says so in terms
that do not let the outsourcing carry the blame the scope decision earns.

**DEC-806 has no dissent attached, and the absence is the record.** Phases 05 and 07 carry minuted dissents
because reasonable people disagreed; nobody disagreed about `MAJ-01`. A version of it could have been argued
as a minor, and 08.05 §6 sets out both why that argument was available and why spending the only currency an
auditee has with a certification body on a finding its own control statement, its own auditor's report and
its own management review minutes already support would have been the wrong trade. **Inventing a
disagreement to match a pattern would have been the dishonest move.**

**DEC-811 and DEC-812 share a date and belong to the same matter.** Both are limbs of `MAJ-01`'s corrective
action, taken on 2027-01-08 as the correction audit closed, and they are recorded in the order the
corrective action states them rather than in the order they were signed. **DEC-812 attaches to `CNB-C-092`
and not to `CNB-C-090`**: `CNB-C-090` is a pre-engagement gate carrying A.5.19 and A.5.21, it contains no
periodic review, and Camberwell — which receives no production data and no system integration — would never
have passed through it. The library's A.5.22 row is `CNB-C-092`. The third limb — the rewritten
programme document — is DEC-808 and was taken three weeks earlier, because it had to exist before the
correction audit could be run against it.

**DEC-810 is the only decision in this phase taken by a risk owner against a proposal that would have suited
the register.** R-08 could have moved three steps to 2 × 3 = 6 and left the register with one fewer
Moderate. Junia Okonkwo stopped it at two, because `CA-06-03`'s corrected emergency change path had six
weeks of evidence behind it at the review date and **six weeks is not a window**. The entry that would have
looked best moving furthest is the entry whose own owner moved it least.

**DEC-813 is a structural decision that had a convenient alternative and was not taken on convenience.**
Section V is the section not covered by the service auditor's opinion, which makes it the section an entity
might prefer for an uncomfortable disclosure. It is also, on the standard's own structure, the only section
`MAJ-01` can go in: it is not a component, commitment, criterion, control or incident of the system, and
clause 9.2 has no trust services criterion for it to be a deviation against. **The reasoning is the same
whichever way the convenience ran**, and what DEC-813 adds beyond the placement is the requirement that
Section V state its own standing on its face rather than leave the reader to know it.

**DEC-815 is the last decision this phase records and it is a decision not to write a sentence.** The
countervailing facts on exception 5 are strong, management believes them, and *management expects an
unmodified opinion* would have cost nothing to write. It is not written. **ADR-0015's rule — nothing
published until it has been proved reachable — applies to a practitioner's conclusion exactly as it applies
to a register band**, and a programme that could tell you on 2027-02-05 what the report will say on
2027-02-26 would be describing something other than an independent examination.

**Two decisions taken in this phase's period are recorded in an earlier phase and are not repeated here.**
DEC-714 moved `CNB-C-120` into November and DEC-715 re-rated R-24 upward; both were taken in October, both
are Phase 07's, and renumbering them into this series to gather the period's decisions in one place would
make two logs harder to read in order to make one of them look complete.
