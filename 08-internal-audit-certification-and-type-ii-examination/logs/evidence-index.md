## Three of these units, and what each of them holds

**EV-804 is a minute of a meeting that went right.** All seven clause 9.3.2 inputs were presented; none was
marked "not presented", so `CNB-C-147`'s gate never had anything to refuse. Input d) 3) carried Camberwell's
report complete with its scope note, to the highest governance body in the ISMS, chaired by the Chief
Executive Officer. **The review's failure was not an absent input. It was what it did with an input that was
present, complete and correct** — and a control that guarantees the agenda cannot guarantee the decision.
The unit is retained unannotated, because a minute with a later explanation attached to it is no longer the
minute that was taken.

**EV-818 holds six units for a control with six occurrences, one of which did not happen.** Phase 06 had to
extend EC-08 to hold a record of a control that did not operate; this is what that extension produced when
somebody finally asked for it. The August unit contains no restore, no snapshot identifier, no row counts and
no checksums, and it is not empty: it holds the deficiency record of 2026-09-14, `CA-06-01`, and the reasoning
for refusing to re-perform and back-date. **A store that can produce a complete negative in two days is a
store that will not be tempted to produce an incomplete positive in ten.**

**EV-824 holds two records where a careless index would hold one.** The population is CloudNimbus's
statement of what happened — 312 changes, 40 provisioning requests, 47 revocations, 6 restore tests. The
selection is Ashcombe &amp; Doyle's. Holding them as one record would obscure the only thing that makes a
test a test: **an entity that produces its own sample has produced its own evidence about its own
controls.**

## What there is no artefact for

**There is no management assertion, no representation letter under O12, no description of the system in
final form, no report, no Section I, no bridge letter and no distribution record.** All of them are Phase
09's and none of them exists on 2027-02-05. **The report is planned for issue on 2027-02-26.**

**There is no artefact recording the service auditor's evaluation of any exception, or of the Stage 2
collision as contradictory evidence.** That work belongs to Ashcombe &amp; Doyle, its output is the report,
and CloudNimbus holds the status meeting minutes at EV-825 and nothing else about it. **A programme that
held a document setting out what its auditor had concluded, before its auditor had issued anything, would be
holding a document somebody had written on the auditor's behalf.**

**There is no artefact for `CNB-C-150` operating**, because it has not operated and will not until the 2028
audit programme is set — `IS-36`.

**There is no artefact for the first surveillance audit**, which falls within twelve months of the
certification decision of 2027-01-20 and is scheduled for **2027-11** under O11.

**And the two final subservice organisation bridge letters are requested and not received in the form the
window needs.** `IS-31` and `CA-07-06` carry the position; the letters that exist are the ones EV-719
recorded in Phase 07, covering to 2026-10-31 for AWS and to the same date for Halcyon Identity.
