## 1. What this phase is

Every earlier phase in this programme built something and then reported on how it behaved. **This is the
phase in which four independent parties report on it instead.** Camberwell Risk Partners audited the
information security management system under clause 9.2. Northgate Certification Services audited it at
Stage 1 and again at Stage 2. Ironwood Security Labs tested the platform for the second time in five months.
Ashcombe &amp; Doyle LLP examined the controls management describes against the trust services criteria for
a six-month period that had, by then, ended.

They produced four registers of findings, in three vocabularies, about one organisation, inside twenty
weeks. **None of the four registers is a subset of another and none of them converts into another**, and
keeping them apart is the discipline this phase is built on.

**It is also the phase in which the programme's own worst finding arrives from outside.** A major
nonconformity was raised against clause 9.2 on 2026-12-02. CloudNimbus had the fact in its own records
**eleven months earlier — that clock runs from January 2026**, when `01.11` §7 recorded CAL-14's coverage as
undefined and raised `PR-06`, to the finding of 2026-12-02. It was read again in September, agreed with,
minuted at the highest governance body in the ISMS and deferred twice. Nothing was missed. It was decided.

**The document identifiers in this phase carry `2027-` rather than `2026-`**, because the identifier scheme
records the year of issue and this phase was issued in 2027. The series does not restart: `CNB-TRUST-2027-800`
follows `CNB-TRUST-2026-713` and `DEC-801` follows `DEC-715`. That is said here once and nowhere else.

## 3. What Phase 08 found, said first

Five things, named here and argued elsewhere. Nothing in this section is restated in full anywhere in this
README.

- **ISO Stage 2 produced 1 major nonconformity, 4 minor nonconformities and 7 opportunities for improvement.** The major, `MAJ-01`, is clause 9.2 and A.5.22 in one failure: the internal audit programme covered the 93 Annex A controls in the Statement of Applicability and did not cover clauses 4 to 10 or `eu-central-1`. It was accepted at the closing meeting without contest. — **08.05**, **08.06**
- **The Type II examination produced 9 test exceptions**, every one of them found by CloudNimbus before it was found by the service auditor, and on 2027-01-28 the engagement team raised that it was considering whether one of them required a modification of the opinion. — **08.11**
- **An ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certificate was issued on 2027-01-22 by Northgate Certification Services, Ltd.**, a certification body accredited by ANAB under ISO/IEC 17021-1 and ISO/IEC 27006-1:2024, valid to 2030-01-21. **ISO does not issue it and has no view on it.** — **08.07**
- **The risk register closed at 38 entries — 0 High · 17 Moderate · 21 Low.** Nineteen entries moved at the review of 2026-12-29, seven were held, twelve already-Low entries did not move. Nothing was closed and nothing was removed. — **08.08**
- **The opinion is not stated.** Fieldwork closed on this vantage; the evaluation of what the deviations mean is the service auditor's and is not complete. **No document in this phase says what the report will say.** — **08.11 §6**, **08.13 §7**

Read **08.01**, **08.02** and **08.03** in order and then **08.05**: they are the same fact arriving four

## 8. Standing framing

**A SOC 2 examination produces an opinion and never a certificate.** The deliverable is a **restricted-use**
examination report carrying a CPA firm's opinion, intended for management, for user entities during the
period, and for other specified parties with sufficient knowledge and understanding. It is not a
general-use document. Nothing in this phase describes CloudNimbus as certified against it, and nothing
states what the opinion will be.

**ISO does not certify anyone.** The certificate of 2027-01-22 was issued by **Northgate Certification
Services, Ltd.**, accredited by **ANAB** under **ISO/IEC 17021-1** and **ISO/IEC 27006-1:2024**.

**Points of focus are not requirements. Annex A is not the requirements**; conformity is assessed against
clauses 4 to 10, with Annex A reached through clause 6.1.3 c) as a comparison verifying that no necessary
control has been omitted.

**"Nonconformity" is ISO and "exception" or "deviation" is SOC 2.** This phase produces both, in quantity,
in the same weeks, about the same organisation. They are never swapped and the finding log carries the
column that keeps them apart.

**Neither deliverable is a determination of compliance with any law**, and no document here reaches a legal
conclusion. No fine, penalty or fee amount appears anywhere. All content is fictional and illustrative.
