## 1. What Phase 06 established

Phase 05 closed by saying that one month is not a period. Phase 06 has three, and the harder thing it has
is not the extra sixty-one days — it is that something went wrong inside them.

**The programme met a service commitment for two months and missed it in the third.** July closed at
99.98% and August at 99.97% against the 99.9% monthly commitment in **SC-01**; September closed at
**99.84%** on seventy-one minutes of unavailability against a monthly allowance of 43.2 minutes. The mean
of the three months is 99.93%, and **the mean is not the test** — 06.01 §3 sets out why in full. The
arithmetic of the 15:05 breach point is stated once, at **06.05 §4.1**. The month-end figure confirmed
something already certain.

**The recovery objectives were met and measured.** The CAL-10 exercise on **2026-08-19** failed the
platform from `us-east-1` to `us-west-2`, achieving **RTO 2h51m against four hours and RPO 4m12s against
fifteen minutes**, both inside **SR-10**. Six findings, one material; **all five of the closed findings were
closed by 2026-09-11**, and `DR-F6` is open and scheduled.

**And twenty days later the platform suffered an in-region failure the exercise could not have found.** A
regional failover replaces the whole application tier, so it never exercises a live application tier's
reaction to a data-layer failover beneath it. The RDS failover on 8 September completed in forty-seven
seconds, inside design; the connection pools held open connections to the demoted instance, the pool health
check tested reachability rather than writability, and reads succeeded while writes failed for seventy-one
minutes.

**The mechanism worked and the assumption about it did not.** That is the deliberate echo of R-37, where
the isolation architecture was sound and the thing that failed was a belief about it, and it is the sentence
this phase would keep if it could keep only one.

worded as 06.12 words them.

| ID | Control | Finding |
|---|---|---|

| `D-06-02` | `CNB-C-096` | `CNB-C-096`'s probe performs a read, so no burn registered against the error budget through a 71-minute write outage — a design deficiency, not an operating failure |
| `D-06-03` | `CNB-C-108` | One reconciliation exception cleared in four business days against a two-business-day service level |

**Separately: SC-01, 99.9% monthly availability excluding announced maintenance windows, not met in
September 2026.** None of the 71 minutes fell in an announced window. Service credits were applied under
the master services agreement to affected tenants on request. The commitment failure is listed with
the deviations and separated from them, because a control that did not operate as described and a promise
to a customer that was not kept are different findings evaluated against different things by different
people.

**Four of the five carry clause 10.2 corrective actions open at this vantage** — **CA-06-01** on the missed
restore test, **CA-06-02** on `CNB-C-108`'s service level, **CA-06-03** on the emergency change path, and
**CA-06-04** on the `CNB-C-136` occurrence that did not happen. `D-06-02` is the fifth and carries none:
a control whose design was inadequate is corrected by amending the statement, and an amendment is a
**correction** rather than a corrective action. The deviation log shows the clause 10.2 test applied to
each and the working behind that answer.

**None of the six is a conclusion about the service auditor's opinion, and Phase 06 does not offer one.**
Deviations are disclosed in Section IV with the auditor's evaluation alongside them, and the auditor
modifies the opinion only where the deviations mean a criterion was not met. That work has not been done.
This is ADR-0015's rule — no forecast published until it has been proved reachable — applied to an opinion
instead of a register.

## 3. What was not done, and why it was not done

**The August restore test was not re-performed and back-dated.** ADR-0026 records the reasoning in one
line: a test performed in September is not evidence of a control operating in August. **The honest number
is the one the service auditor will compute.** The team's error is recorded as such — the calendar named
CAL-09 and CAL-10 in the same month and nobody owned the question of whether one discharged the other. It
does not: **a replica that has faithfully replicated corruption passes CAL-10 and fails CAL-09.**

**A.5.30 was not upgraded, because it did not need to be — and that is the finding.** A.5.30 reads
**`Implemented`** on the current issue of the Statement of Applicability and has since the **2026-06-15**
reissue, at which 27 of the 29 Partial or Planned entries closed. It was moved there **before `CNB-C-100`
had operated**: CAL-10 was two months away, and the status ran ahead of its evidence, which is the failure
mode clause 7.5 exists to prevent and the failure R-30 describes. **At 2026-08-19 the testing limb acquired
the evidence the status had been asserting since June.** The status is now supportable where it was not,
and nothing anybody did in September made it so. Two further facts are carried openly: **`CNB-C-136`, the
sole carrier of A.5.30's planning limb, did not operate in the one month the window gave it** — `D-06-05` —
and **two of A.5.30's four limbs have no row in the library citing them at all** — the implementation limb
and the maintenance limb — recorded as `IS-24` and referred to the clause 9.2 internal audit rather than
closed by an edit.

**No new risk was admitted.** ADR-0029 sets out why: R-18 already describes an availability incident
breaching the monthly commitment, and a second entry for the same exposure would be double-counting. **A
register that grows every time something happens is a log, not a register.**

**Two defects in the published library were recorded rather than quietly corrected.** The first is the two
post-incident review windows carried by `CNB-C-102` (five business days) and `CNB-C-075` (ten) for the same
artefact — `IS-20`. The second is A.5.30's **implementation and maintenance limbs**, neither of which any
row in the library cites — `IS-24`. Both were referred to the clause 9.2 internal audit rather than edited, because **a library that
corrects itself silently cannot demonstrate that it was ever wrong**, and IS-24 is the harder of the two to
leave alone: adding a citation to a row that already exists would make the gap disappear without anything
about the ICT readiness having changed.

`CNB-C-096` was treated differently and **amended**, because it was not in disagreement with anything and
nothing was missing from the library: it was silent about what a probe must exercise where `CNB-C-068`
beside it was explicit, and a statement that is silent can be completed.

## 4. The register

**The register entered the September CAL-06 review at 37 entries — 7 High · 18 Moderate · 12 Low** — and
left it at **37 entries — 8 High · 17 Moderate · 12 Low.** 8 + 17 + 12 = 37.

**Six reductions were proposed and none was accepted. One entry moved, upward, and nobody had proposed
it.**

**R-08** — a change is deployed to production without recorded peer review — moved from 4 × 3 = 12 to
**5 × 3 = 15, Moderate to High**. Nine of the quarter's 160 changes reached production without a code-owner
review at merge, by the designed emergency path, and `03.02` §2 puts likelihood 5 at "occurring now, or
expected more than once a year". It moves on likelihood; the consequence is unchanged.

**R-08 is the register's first likelihood-5 entry.** `03.04` §5's likelihood distribution recorded **zero**
at 5 — the anchor existed and nothing had ever reached it, R-08 among the entries that sat at 4 because that
was what the evidence then supported. The top of the scale is now occupied, by a counted frequency rather
than by a judgement.

Because the residual is now **High**, `03.02` §6 required **the Chief Executive Officer, Elise Fontaine,
with the reasoning minuted**, over and above risk owner **Junia Okonkwo's** clause 6.1.3 f) acceptance.
`03.02` §6 requires two things of a retained High residual, and only one of them is done: CAL-03's Q3
occurrence was held on **2026-09-24**, five days before the register review, so Elise Fontaine's decision
of 2026-09-29 could not have been reported to the Audit &amp; Risk Committee.

> **Half of it fired.** The retention decision is taken and minuted; **the reporting limb is not complete at
> this vantage** and completes at the Audit &amp; Risk Committee's Q4 report. A rule that requires two things
> and has done one is a rule that has done one, and this phase is not going to write it up as though the
> second had happened. Carried as **`PR-40`** in the RAID log, owner Karim Haddad, due at CAL-03's Q4
> occurrence.

Clause 10.2 corrective action **`CA-06-03`** is open against the emergency change path.

**`03.04` is not amended, because a baseline that is rewritten every time the register moves is not a
baseline.** It is the dated position at 2026-04-10 and its value to the examination is that it has not
changed. The current position is carried by the register workbook and by the CAL-06 review records, which
is where a reader should be sent and where GOV-24 sends them.

R-18, R-19, R-21, R-30, R-04 and R-14 were all declined on their own facts. **R-21's decline is the one to
read**: the reduction rested partly on the June forensic search, and that search is the event R-21 describes
rather than evidence against it — the thirteen-month query horizon could not reach nine months of a
twenty-two-month exposure, and it concluded before 2026-06-22, when DEC-508 reviewed the retention gap it
exposed, nine days before the window opened. What remained was one
reconstruction, and one event is not a period. Held at 3 × 3 = 9. The population did not change — nothing
closed, nothing removed, as at every vantage since the baseline.

**A quarterly review at which nothing was reduced is not a review that found nothing.** It is a review whose
evidence was three months old in a six-month window, at an organisation that had just missed a commitment.

## 5. Three amendments, and two re-issues

This phase amends two things published in Phase 04 and one published in Phase 02. **Both phases are
re-issued** as a consequence.

| What changed | Why | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| **`CNB-C-096`** now requires the synthetic probe to perform a **write**, with the read probe retained as a separate signal | The control operated exactly as designed through all seventy-one minutes and reported healthy, because the probe performed a read. Amending the statement is the only correction available for a design deficiency | **DEC-609**, 2026-09-15; deployed 2026-09-19 as `ACT-06-02`. **Phase 04 re-issued** |
| **04.11's cadence commitment** is corrected to name **CAL-03 and CAL-07 to CAL-09**, with **CAL-06 deliberately late** | A register review evaluates a quarter's accumulated evidence; one held in the first fortnight of a quarter reviews the previous quarter's evidence under the wrong label. **CAL-06 has operated late in its quarter wherever a register existed to review — 2026-06-15 and 2026-09-29** — the 2026-03 occurrence having reviewed the risk assessment *method* rather than a register, and 2026-06-15 having been scheduled to **MS-09** rather than to the end of the quarter. CAL-07 and CAL-08 certify a current state and are properly scheduled early — though CAL-07's Q3 occurrence was in fact week four, recorded at 06.11 §5 as an observation | **DEC-602**, 2026-09-02. **Phase 04 re-issued** |
| **`02.12`'s statement of SC-01** now reads "99.9% monthly availability of the platform, **excluding maintenance windows announced at least fourteen days in advance**" | `02.12` published the commitment without the exclusion the master services agreement contains. **A 99.9% figure measured with an undisclosed exclusion is not the figure the customer reads**, and a description of the system carrying the commitment without the exclusion is incomplete under **DC2** | **DEC-613**, 2026-09-24, Marisol Vega. **Phase 02 re-issued** |

The middle one is the smallest change and the most instructive. **A commitment published in one phase and
quietly not honoured in a later one is the shape of finding an auditor produces from a five-minute
comparison of two documents.** Correcting it costs a paragraph.

The third is the most uncomfortable, and it is the only amendment in this portfolio that changes what a
**customer** was told rather than what an auditor would be shown. It surfaced because the CAL-10 exercise
ran inside an announced maintenance window — **06:00 to 10:00 UTC on 2026-08-19, four hours, notified to
every customer on 2026-08-05** — and **23 customer-visible minutes** fell inside it and were excluded from
August's measurement, while `02.12` published SC-01 with no exclusion at all. **The exclusion was engaged
twice in the quarter, not once**: TP-20's availability architecture change was delivered on **2026-07-15**
in a window notified **2026-07-01**, consuming **4 excluded minutes**. An exclusion the programme relies on
twice in three months is not an edge case.

**Twenty-three minutes did not change August's result, and four did not change July's.** The omission in
`02.12` would have changed a reader's understanding of every month in the report, which is why it was
corrected over twenty-seven minutes rather than left until a month when it mattered. **A carve-out is worth
disclosing in the quarter it is trivial, because the quarter it is not trivial is the quarter nobody will
believe the disclosure.** An exclusion that only appears when it is needed is not an exclusion, it is an
excuse.

## 9. What could still go wrong

**Three months is half a period.** Every clean figure here is a figure about ninety-two days. `CNB-C-098`
has three occurrences left and one already missing; `CNB-C-112` has one left; `CNB-C-113` and `CNB-C-103`
have none left in the window; `CNB-C-073` and `CNB-C-076` have their only one still to come, in Q4; and
`CNB-C-136`'s single occurrence is already gone. **The rate the service auditor computes will be computed
over a population that does not exist yet**, and it can move in either direction.

**`DR-F6` is open** — failback was not timed on 19 August and took place unmeasured over the two following
days. It is scheduled against the Q4 game day and due 2026-11-30, and it is the one finding the exercise
itself did not answer.

**`CA-06-02` is unresolved.** `CNB-C-108` carries a two-business-day service level and a weekly Tuesday
queue, and for **three days of every seven** the second cannot deliver the first. An exception raised on a
**Tuesday after the review** reaches business day two on Thursday and the next queue on the following
Tuesday, business day five; a **Wednesday** exception reaches the queue on business day four; a **Thursday**
exception is the worked example, at business day three. Three of seven, not two — **`CA-06-02`'s exposure is
half again what an earlier count of it stated**. Until the corrective action answers which of the two is
wrong, the control will keep producing the same deviation on the same days of the week.

**`CA-06-03` is open on an entry that is now High.** The emergency change path put nine changes into
production without a code-owner review at merge, which moved R-08 to 5 × 3 = 15 and required the Chief
Executive Officer's minuted retention. Until the corrective action lands, the departure from **SR-11**
described at 06.12 §5.3 recurs every time the path is used, and the path exists to be used.

**`CA-06-04` is open on a control with no occurrences left.** `CNB-C-136`'s re-scheduled 2026-08 occurrence
did not happen and was not back-dated, so A.5.30's planning limb has no evidence inside the window at all —
and the Statement of Applicability reads `Implemented`. Nothing before 2026-12-31 changes that.

**And the evidence still has to survive the window.** ML-1 recorded a control whose design was adequate and
whose evidence trail was not, and every claim in this phase resolves later to whether the exercise report,
the reconciliation records, the triage dispositions, the restore test outputs and the register review
minutes are in the evidence store in the unit 04.12 defined. **Remediating a control is not the same as
remediating the evidence trail it leaves behind.**
