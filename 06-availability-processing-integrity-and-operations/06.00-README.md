## 1. What this phase is

Phase 04 published the availability and processing integrity controls, `CNB-C-094` to `CNB-C-113`, and said
of all four non-security categories that they had **never been examined**. Phase 05 described the security
estate one month into the observation window and argued that one month is not a period.

**Phase 06 is the first phase in this portfolio that has something to report which nobody planned.** Three
months of the six-month window have run. In them the platform performed a full regional disaster recovery
exercise and met both recovery objectives; twenty days later it suffered a Severity-1 incident that took
writes down for seventy-one minutes, missed a monthly service commitment for the first time in the
programme's life, and was not detected by the control that exists to detect exactly that.

The phase is therefore an operating record and the account of one bad afternoon, written while the facts
were still fresh — because the incidents that go missing from a description of a system are the ones nobody
wrote down at the time.

## 2. The vantage — 2026-09-30

**Phase 06 speaks as at 2026-09-30**, three months into the observation window that opened on 2026-07-01
and closes on 2026-12-31. Phases 01 and 02 wrote from 2026-03-31, Phase 03 from 2026-04-30, Phase 04 from
2026-06-30 and Phase 05 from 2026-07-31.

These have happened and are described in the past tense: the **CAL-10 disaster recovery exercise of
2026-08-19** and its six findings; the **Severity-1 incident of 2026-09-08**, `INC-2026-031`; the operation
of every availability, processing integrity, change, monitoring and incident control across July, August
and September; and the **CAL-06 quarterly risk register review of 2026-09-29**.

**Nothing after 2026-09-30 is stated as an outcome.** There has been no Stage 1 and no Stage 2, no
examination fieldwork, no sample drawn, no test exception evaluated by the service auditor, no nonconformity
raised by the certification body, no opinion and no certificate. The **CAL-16 business continuity exercise**,
the **second penetration test** and the **quarterly in-region failover game day** are scheduled and have not
been performed. Half the window is still ahead, which is why this phase reports `CNB-C-098` as **2 of 3
occurrences to date** and states no window deviation rate: three of the six occurrences have not happened,
and a rate computed over a population that does not yet exist is a number invented to look precise.

**One exclusion is deliberate.** The clause 9.2 internal audit ran 2026-09-14 to 2026-09-25 and the clause
9.3 management review was held 2026-09-30, both inside this phase's period. **The operating record and the
disclosures set out in this phase were supplied to the internal audit as auditee evidence**, and everything
else about either event belongs to Phase 08 and appears nowhere here.

## 3. What Phase 06 found, said first

**SC-01, the 99.9% monthly availability commitment, was not met in September 2026.** Availability is
measured and reported **per region**, which is the unit EC-09 samples. In `us-east-1` July closed at 99.98%
and August at 99.97%, and September closed at **99.84%** on seventy-one minutes of unavailability against a
monthly allowance of 43.2 minutes; `eu-central-1` closed at 100% in each of the three months and `us-west-2`
carries no production traffic. **SC-01 was met for the 41 EU-residency customers and missed for the 599
served from `us-east-1`** — a blended platform figure would have concealed that, and 06.01 says so at
length. The three-month mean for `us-east-1` is 99.93%, and **the mean is not the test**; 06.01 owns that
argument and states it in full.

Five control deviations were recorded in the quarter, and one service commitment failure, which is a
different kind of thing and is listed separately. The deviation table is printed once in full, in 06.12;
what follows is the five identifiers with one line each.

- **`D-06-01`** · `CNB-C-098` · The August monthly restore test was not performed · 1 of 3 occurrences to date
- **`D-06-02`** · `CNB-C-096` · The probe performs a read, so a seventy-one-minute write outage registered no burn against the error budget — a design deficiency, not an operating failure · 1 event
- **`D-06-03`** · `CNB-C-108` · One reconciliation exception cleared in four business days against a two-business-day service level · 1 of 26
- **`D-06-04`** · `CNB-C-082` · Two emergency changes retrospectively approved on the sixth and seventh business day · 2 of 9
- **`D-06-05`** · `CNB-C-136` · The re-scheduled 2026-08 occurrence of the annual ICT continuity re-derivation was not performed · 1 of 1 occurrence in the window

Separately from those five: **SC-01, the 99.9% monthly availability commitment, was not met in `us-east-1`
in September 2026 — one month of three.** A commitment failure is not a control deviation and is not counted
as one.

**None of these six is a conclusion about the service auditor's opinion, and Phase 06 does not offer one.**
Deviations are disclosed with the auditor's evaluation of them; the auditor modifies an opinion only where
the deviations mean a criterion was not met. That evaluation has not happened and cannot be forecast, which
is ADR-0015's rule applied to an opinion instead of to a risk register.

**The only movement at the September review was a rating going up.** The **CAL-06 review of 2026-09-29**
opened at 37 entries — 7 High, 18 Moderate, 12 Low — took **six reduction proposals and accepted none of
them**, and then re-rated one entry nobody had proposed: **R-08**, a change reaching production without
recorded peer review, from 4 × 3 = 12 to **5 × 3 = 15**, Moderate to High, because nine occurrences in
ninety-two days meets `03.02` §2's likelihood-5 anchor. **R-21 did not move.** The register closed at
**37 entries — 8 High, 17 Moderate, 12 Low**. **No new risk was admitted for the September incident**,
because R-18 already describes it — ADR-0029.

**A quarterly review at which nothing was reduced is not a review that found nothing.** It is a review whose
evidence was three months old in a six-month window, at an organisation that had just missed a commitment.

## 8. Standing framing

Nothing in this phase is a determination of compliance with any law. The SOC 2 deliverable is a
**restricted-use** examination report carrying a CPA firm's **opinion**; there is no such thing as a SOC 2
certification and nothing here describes CloudNimbus as certified against it. The ISO deliverable is a
**certificate issued by an accredited certification body**, Northgate Certification Services, Ltd.,
accredited by ANAB — not by ISO, which certifies nobody. **Points of focus are not requirements** and
**Annex A is not the requirements**; conformity is assessed against clauses 4 to 10. A SOC 2 finding is a
**deviation** or **exception** and an ISO finding is a **nonconformity**, and this phase produces the first
kind, and internally identified nonconformities handled under clause 10.2. None has been raised by the
certification body, which has not audited. Service credits were applied under the master services agreement
and **no amount is stated anywhere**. All content is fictional and illustrative.
