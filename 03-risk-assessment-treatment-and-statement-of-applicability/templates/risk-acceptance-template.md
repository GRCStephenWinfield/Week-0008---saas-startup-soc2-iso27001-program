For risks treated by **retention** under clause 6.1.3 a), and for residual risk accepted under clause
6.1.3 f). Retention is a decision. It is not a category for risks nobody wanted to own.

| Field | Guidance |
|---|---|
| Risk | The `R-nn` |
| Current rating | Likelihood × impact = score, and the band |
| Why it is retained | One of: **immovable** — the next likelihood step is 1 and the scenario is foreseeable; **on the floor** — a 2 × 4 stops at eight; **treatment costs more than the risk**; or **the treatment is forbidden by a commitment**, which must name the commitment |
| What was considered and rejected | The treatments available and why each was not taken. Retention with no rejected alternatives is retention nobody examined |
| Accepter — clause 6.1.3 f) | **The risk owner.** The clause names the risk owners and nobody else, and an executive acceptance recorded in place of the owner's is a nonconformity waiting to be raised |
| Additional acceptance | The signature CloudNimbus's own criteria require at that band, **on top of the owner's and never instead of it**. Any entry carrying impact 5 requires the Chief Executive Officer regardless of band — a band is a summary, and an impact of 5 is a statement about what happens to somebody |
| Date accepted | |
| Review | At the management review under CAL-15, and at the quarterly register review under CAL-06 |
| What would reopen it | The change in circumstances that would require the acceptance to be revisited |

**The last field is the one that stops an acceptance becoming permanent by default.** R-20 is retained
because obligation O8 forbids the cross-region recovery that would reduce it; if a customer's residency
requirement changed, or if an in-region recovery capability became available, the acceptance would have to
be taken again rather than assumed to continue.

**On R-20 specifically.** It is the clearest case in the register of a commitment to a customer creating a
risk to that same customer. CloudNimbus cannot honour both residency and cross-region recovery, has chosen
residency, and records the consequence rather than describing the choice as risk management.
