## Identifier series established in this phase

| Series | Range | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `CNB-TRUST-2026-3NN` | 300–313 | Phase 03 document IDs |
| **`R-nn`** | **01–36** | **The information security risk register.** Reserved since Phase 01 and opened here |
| `TH-nn` | 01–14 | Threat sources |
| `TP-nn` | 01–34 | Risk treatment plan items |
| `DEC-3nn` | 301–312 | Phase 03 decisions |
| `ADR-nnnn` | 0011–0015 | Architecture decision records |
| `EV-3nn` | 301–310 | Phase 03 evidence artefacts |
| `PR/AS/IS/DP` | continued | RAID entries — **delivery risk, and never rated** |

`PR-nn` and `R-nn` are different registers and are deliberately kept apart. A reader who conflates them will
find delivery entries that appear to have escaped the scoring scale.

Every workbook is generated by parsing the narrative markdown. The build **re-derives every band count and
the entire close forecast** from the register and fails if the published figures do not follow. A workbook
cannot disagree with the prose, and the prose cannot disagree with its own arithmetic.
