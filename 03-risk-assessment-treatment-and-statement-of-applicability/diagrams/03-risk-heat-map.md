**Bands: High ≥ 15 · Moderate 8–12 · Low ≤ 6.** Read the empty cells as well as the full ones.

**Column L = 1 is empty**, and that is the scale working as intended: likelihood 1 is reserved for the not
reasonably foreseeable, and nothing in a live SaaS platform's register qualifies.
**Column L = 5 is empty** because nothing in the register is occurring now or expected more than annually.
**Row I = 1 is empty** because a risk whose consequence is negligible did not earn a place in the register.

The products of two integers in 1–5 are {1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 9, 10, 12, 15, 16, 20, 25}. **Seven, eleven,
thirteen and fourteen cannot occur**, so the band boundaries at 6/8 and 12/15 fall in gaps and no rating is
ever ambiguous about which band it is in. That is a property of the scale, not a coincidence, and it is why
the thresholds were set where they were.
