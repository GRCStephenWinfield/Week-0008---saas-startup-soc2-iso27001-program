## Context

Clause 6.1.3 a) offers four treatment options: modify, retain, share and avoid. A treatment plan showing all four in use looks more considered than one showing three.

## Decision

**Avoid: 0.** Thirty-one modified, four retained, one shared, none avoided.

## Consequences

The plan is one line shorter and one degree more honest. Anybody reading it can see that CloudNimbus did not withdraw geolocation, did not stop recording leave type and did not exit the European Union — and can therefore trust the other thirty-six lines a little more.

## Alternatives considered and rejected

Recording the decision not to build a proposed raw-geolocation export as an avoidance — rejected. It was never on the register, so avoiding it avoided nothing that was ever measured, and a treatment plan that records the avoidance of an unregistered risk has learned to fill in a form.
