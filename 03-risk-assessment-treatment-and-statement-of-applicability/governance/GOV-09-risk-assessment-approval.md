## 3. Baseline admitted

**7 High · 17 Moderate · 12 Low = 36.** Every entry carries a
named owner drawn from the people above, a threat source from TH-01 to TH-14, and a rating that is the
product of its likelihood and its impact.

## 4. The four things the committee was asked to accept, not merely to note

**That impact does not move.** Treatment makes events less likely; it rarely makes them less damaging when
they happen. The committee accepted that no future report would show progress by reducing an impact score.

**That likelihood 1 is unavailable.** It is reserved for the not reasonably foreseeable, and the column is
empty at baseline. It is not the rating a treated risk arrives at.

**That eight is a floor.** Twelve entries carry impact 4 or 5 and can therefore never be rated Low, whatever
is done to them. The committee was given that list at the point of approval rather than discovering it
later.

**That the register is not closed.** DEC-306 admits thirty-six entries and provides for additions on
evidence. A register that cannot grow is a register that has stopped listening.

## 5. Recorded concentration

Ten distinct risk owners carry the thirty-six entries, and **Wes Delacroix carries ten of them**. The
committee recorded the concentration rather than redistributing entries to make the table look balanced.
Ownership follows the ability to act, and infrastructure is where much of the register lives.
