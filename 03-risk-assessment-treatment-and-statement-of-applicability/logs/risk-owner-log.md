## The concentration, recorded rather than smoothed

The distribution is uneven and the unevenness is real. Infrastructure and platform engineering carry most of
the register because most of the register is about infrastructure and platform engineering. Redistributing
entries to flatten the table would have produced owners who could not act on what they owned, which is the
only failure mode of risk ownership that matters.

What the concentration does create is a **single point of dependency in the treatment plan**: a large share
of TP-01 to TP-34 falls to a small number of people, all of whom have day jobs, inside a programme resourced
at 4.6 full-time equivalents. That is recorded as PR-18 and was put to the Trust Committee at GOV-09 §5
rather than discovered when the dates began to slip.

**Clause 6.1.3 f) names the risk owner and nobody else**, so the owner's acceptance is the one the clause
requires and it is never substituted. CloudNimbus's own criteria then add a second signature: any entry
carrying **impact 5** requires acceptance by the Chief Executive Officer regardless of its band, which is
why R-19 and R-20 — both Moderate — carry Elise Fontaine's countersignature while R-24 and R-28 carry
Karim Haddad's. A band is a
summary; an impact of 5 is a statement about what happens to somebody, and the summary should not be allowed
to hide it.
