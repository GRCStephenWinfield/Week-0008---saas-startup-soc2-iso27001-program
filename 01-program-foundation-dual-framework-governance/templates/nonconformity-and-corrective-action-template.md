For ISO/IEC 27001 clause 10.2. **This template is for nonconformities, which are an ISO concept.** A SOC 2
test exception is a different thing under a different regime and is tracked separately; do not file one on
this form, and do not use the words interchangeably.

Clause 10.2 requires more than a fix. It requires the organisation to react to the nonconformity and deal
with the consequences, then to evaluate whether action is needed to eliminate the **cause** so that it does
not recur, and to determine whether similar nonconformities exist or could potentially occur elsewhere.
That last obligation is the one most often skipped.

| Field | Guidance |
|---|---|
| NC ID | `NC-nnn` |
| Source | Internal audit · Stage 1 · Stage 2 · surveillance · management review · self-identified |
| Grade | Major · Minor. An opportunity for improvement is **not** a nonconformity and does not use this form |
| Clause or control | The requirement not conformed with |
| Statement of nonconformity | What was found, factually, with the evidence |
| **Correction** | What was done immediately to deal with the nonconformity and its consequences |
| **Cause analysis** | Why it happened. "Human error" is not a cause |
| **Corrective action** | What eliminates the cause so it does not recur |
| **Extent review** | Whether similar nonconformities exist elsewhere or could occur elsewhere. Required by clause 10.2 b) 3) — record the answer even where it is "no", with the basis |
| Owner and target date | Named individual |
| Effectiveness verification | How and when the corrective action was confirmed effective, by whom |
| Closure | Date and verifier |

A major nonconformity blocks certification until correction and corrective action have been submitted and
verified. A minor nonconformity does not block, but leaving one open through a surveillance cycle is a
reliable route to it being escalated.
