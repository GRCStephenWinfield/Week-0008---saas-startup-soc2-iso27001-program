For ISO/IEC 27001 clause 9.3. The management review is chaired by the CEO and is **not** the monthly Trust
Committee. Clause 9.3.2 specifies the inputs and clause 9.3.3 specifies the outputs, and a review that
omits an input is a nonconformity regardless of how well the meeting went.

## Outputs — clause 9.3.3

Decisions related to continual improvement opportunities and any need for changes to the ISMS. Minute the
decision, the owner and the date. An output recorded as "noted" is not an output.
