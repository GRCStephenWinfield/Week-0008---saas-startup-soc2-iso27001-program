## 5. Points of discussion recorded at the Chair's request

Lorraine Kessler asked whether the objective stated as an unmodified opinion was an objective the company
could hold itself to. The point was accepted: the opinion is the service auditor's and CloudNimbus does not
control it. The charter retains the target and records the qualification in terms. Simone Bracco asked
whether three categories, being the contractual floor, would be sufficient. The answer given was that it
would be, and that the company was choosing to do more; the reasoning is at `01.05`.
