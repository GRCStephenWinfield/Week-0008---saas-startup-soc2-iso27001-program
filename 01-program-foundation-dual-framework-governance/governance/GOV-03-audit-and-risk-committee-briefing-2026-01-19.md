## 1. Purpose

To brief the Audit &amp; Risk Committee on the assurance architecture of the trust programme, and to obtain
the committee's acceptance of accountability for the internal audit programme.

## 2. The problem put to the committee

CloudNimbus has 187 employees and no internal audit function. ISO/IEC 27001 clause 9.2 requires the
organisation to conduct internal audits at planned intervals, and requires the auditors to be objective and
impartial. A company of this size cannot staff that internally, and a company of this size also cannot have
the VP Security &amp; Trust audit his own management system.

## 3. The architecture proposed

Four external parties, deliberately separated. Solstice Trust Advisors supports control design and may not
audit its own work. Camberwell Risk Partners performs the clause 9.2 internal audit and may not have
advised on the design of what it audits. Northgate Certification Services certifies and may not consult.
Ashcombe &amp; Doyle LLP examines and may not design, implement or operate the controls it examines.

## 4. The cheaper option, and why it was refused

A single provider offered readiness advisory, an outsourced internal audit function and certification
through an affiliated certification body as one managed engagement, for materially less than the sum of
B-02, B-03 and B-04. It was refused, and the first reason is that it is not permissible: a certification
body may not certify a management system its affiliate helped build. The committee's view, recorded at the Chair's
request, was that the value of an internal audit is entirely a function of its independence, and that a
programme which buys its own assurance from its own advisor has bought nothing.

## 5. What the committee accepted

Accountability for the internal audit programme (A-16) and ratification of the programme charter (A-01).
The committee will receive a quarterly report and will convene out of cycle on any scope change or dated
commitment at risk. Camberwell Risk Partners' engagement terms give it access to the chair without
management present.

The Chair asked that the committee's own limitations be minuted rather than left to be discovered. The
Audit &amp; Risk Committee has **one voting member**, herself. Elise Fontaine, Marisol Vega and Karim Haddad
attend by invitation and are not members; Simone Bracco attends as a non-voting observer. A committee of
one is not a committee in the sense a listed company means, and nothing in this architecture should be
described as though it were. What is actually available to a 187-person private company is a single
non-executive with a direct line to the outsourced internal auditor and the standing to convene without
management in the room. That is thinner than a mature structure, it depends materially on one person, and
it is to be revisited when the board expands.

## 6. The matter the committee flagged for its own record

The committee noted that the internal audit programme is currently described as annual and risk-weighted,
and that its **coverage** is not yet defined. Defining coverage was deferred to the internal audit charter.
The committee asked that the deferral be recorded rather than left implicit, and it is recorded here and at
`01.11`.
