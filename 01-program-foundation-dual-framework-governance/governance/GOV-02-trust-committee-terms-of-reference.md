## 1. Purpose

The Trust Committee is the management forum accountable for delivery of the trust programme. It is not a
board committee and holds no authority the board has not delegated to management.

## 3. Cadence and quorum

Monthly. Quorum is five voting members including the chair or the programme owner. Minutes are issued
within five business days and are evidence for CC2.1 and CC2.2 and for clause 7.4. They are **not**
evidence for CC2.3, which concerns communication with **external** parties: the trust centre, report
delivery under O2 and sub-processor notice under O5 carry that.

## 4. Standing agenda

Progress against MS-01 to MS-18; the risk register; open nonconformities and corrective actions; evidence
completeness against the control library; obligations O1 to O12 falling due; budget against the envelope;
and any matter a member escalates.

## 5. Decision rights

The committee decides within the approved envelope and within the agreed scope. It escalates to the Audit
&amp; Risk Committee any change to the SOC 2 system scope, the ISMS boundary, or a dated commitment to a
customer or an audit body; and to the board anything that would exceed $1,400,000.

## 6. What this committee is deliberately not

It is not the management review required by clause 9.3. The management review is chaired by the CEO,
carries the inputs and outputs clauses 9.3.2 and 9.3.3 specify, and is minuted separately. A monthly
delivery committee is not a substitute for it, and treating it as one is a common route to a clause 9.3
nonconformity.
