## Context

Obligation O1 sets a contractual floor of Security, Availability and Confidentiality — 38 criteria. Processing Integrity and Privacy are optional. Each adds criteria, evidence and cost, and Privacy adds the most of anything available.

## Decision

All five categories are in scope, taking the criteria population to 61. Processing Integrity is included because the calculation engine is the product. Privacy is included because the mobile application collects geolocation directly from the individual at clock-in.

## Consequences

Privacy adds 18 criteria to a 43-criterion baseline, a 42% increase for one category, and requires CloudNimbus to answer P1.1 notice as a party in a direct collection relationship rather than pointing at the employer. Phase 07 carries that work. The examination is materially larger than the contract requires and the difference is a deliberate commercial investment.

## Alternatives considered and rejected

Security, Availability and Confidentiality only — the contractual floor, and the cheapest defensible answer. Rejected. Security and Confidentiality with Privacy deferred to a later period — rejected because deferring notice obligations on data already being collected is not a scoping decision, it is a delay.
