## Context

The Type II observation window runs 2026-07-01 to 2026-12-31. The certification body's availability and the requirement for the ISMS to have operated long enough to produce clause 9 evidence put Stage 2 at 2026-11-30 to 2026-12-04 — inside the window. A nonconformity raised there would sit within the period the service auditor is examining.

## Decision

Accept the collision. Schedule Stage 2 at 2026-11-30 and inform the service auditor in advance that an independent audit of overlapping subject matter will occur inside the period, so that any finding reaches Ashcombe &amp; Doyle as planned information rather than as a discovery.

## Consequences

Any ISO nonconformity raised between 2026-11-30 and 2026-12-31 becomes information the service auditor must evaluate, potentially as contradictory evidence against CC4.1 and CC4.2. That is a real exposure and it is accepted knowingly. The alternative exposures were worse.

## Alternatives considered and rejected

Move Stage 2 into 2027, after the window closes — rejected because it delays the certificate past the point where it earns anything commercially, and because a certification audit deliberately scheduled to avoid an examiner's sight is not a decision that survives being written down. Shorten the observation window to three months so it closes before Stage 2 — rejected because a quarterly control then has a population of one.
