Fourteen artefacts. Phase 01 produces governance evidence, not control evidence: nothing here is testable
for operating effectiveness because nothing here is a control. The distinction matters, because a
foundation phase that presents its own minutes as control evidence has misunderstood what the service
auditor will sample.

Artefacts that evidence a control are indexed **by control** rather than by framework, which is the
mechanical consequence of ADR-0001. The governance artefacts below predate every control in the programme
and are indexed by the phase and the decision they record; they enter the control index only when a later
phase attaches them to one. A framework-indexed store would require every artefact to be filed twice and would guarantee
divergence at the first amendment.
