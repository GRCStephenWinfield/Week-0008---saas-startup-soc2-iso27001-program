Completed for any event that might engage **SC-02** and obligation **O4** — notification of a security
incident affecting customer data within 48 hours of determination.

| Field | Guidance |
|---|---|
| Event | What happened, factually |
| Determination date and time | **The clock starts here.** Record who determined it and on what basis |
| Was customer data affected | Yes, no, or unknown. **Unknown is an answer and must not be written as no** |
| Environment | Production, pre-production, or synthetic. Say which, because it decides the next question |
| Evidence of exploitation | What was searched, what was found — **and the period the search covers** |
| **Period the condition existed** | And therefore **the period the search does not cover**. State the arithmetic |
| Contractual assessment | Against SC-02 and O4 by name, with the conclusion and who reached it |
| Statutory position | **Referred to counsel. This form reaches no legal conclusion** |
| Voluntary disclosure | Considered separately from the contractual question, because the answers can differ |
| Decision, decider, date | |
| Dissent | **If anybody dissented, the substance and not the fact.** A dissent recorded as "one member disagreed" is a dissent nobody can evaluate |
| Revisit condition | What would cause the decision to be taken again |

**On the two period fields.** They exist because of R-37. "No evidence of exploitation" is a statement
about a period, and a reader cannot weigh it without knowing whether that period is the whole of the
exposure or part of it. A form that records the first without the second produces a sentence that is true
and misleading.
